HR Manager — Human Resources

The Opportunity

You'll step into your first people leadership role within HR — building and leading a team of 3-6 HR professionals while staying close to the work that made you great as an individual contributor. This isn't a role where you disappear into meetings and lose touch with the craft. You'll develop your team, shape how they deliver HR programs, and grow into the kind of leader who makes everyone around them better. If you're ready to multiply your impact by investing in others while keeping your hands in meaningful HR work, this is your next move.

What You'll Do

  • Build a team that delivers — You'll lead a small HR team to consistently hit commitments with quality, creating clear ownership and accountability while fostering the kind of psychological safety where people raise concerns early and learn from mistakes.
  • Develop your people — You'll run effective 1:1s, deliver feedback that actually lands, and create development plans that help your team members grow their skills and careers within HR. Their growth is your primary metric.
  • Translate strategy into team action — You'll take HR function goals and turn them into clear team objectives that everyone understands, with effective workload distribution and realistic timelines — communicating early when things shift.
  • Stay technically credible — You'll maintain your domain expertise and step in when your team needs support — whether that's navigating a tricky hiring manager conversation, interpreting compensation policy, or resolving a sensitive employee query. You teach, not just solve.
  • Manage delivery across HR cycles — You'll plan and prioritize your team's work through performance reviews, compensation cycles, and compliance deadlines, proactively managing dependencies and keeping stakeholders informed on progress and capacity.

What You Bring

  • 5-8 years of HR experience across one or more disciplines (Talent Acquisition, HR Operations, HRBP, L&D, or Compensation & Benefits)
  • Strong individual contributor track record with recognition for quality, reliability, and sound judgment
  • Informal leadership or mentorship experience — you've helped other HR professionals grow, onboarded new teammates, or led project workstreams
  • Working knowledge of employment law, compliance, and regulatory requirements relevant to your domain
  • Genuine interest in people leadership — you want to manage because you care about developing others, not just because it's the next rung
  • Clear communication skills — you can set expectations, deliver feedback, and represent your team's work to business leaders and senior HR leadership
  • Experience working through full HR cycles: performance reviews, compensation planning, open enrollment, or equivalent cyclical work

Nice to Have

  • Familiarity with HRIS systems and HR analytics tools
  • HR certifications (SHRM-CP, PHR)
  • Experience working across multiple HR disciplines, not just one

Why [Company]

  • Invest in your leadership growth — We know this might be your first management role, and we're set up to support that. You'll receive coaching from senior HR leaders, participate in management development programs, and have a safe environment to learn through real challenges.
  • HR that matters — Our HR function is a strategic partner to the business, not a compliance back office. The work your team does directly impacts how our people experience their careers here.
  • A function that practices what it preaches — We hold ourselves to the same standards of people management we ask of the rest of the organization. Psychological safety, career development, and honest feedback are the norm, not the aspiration.

How to Apply

Submit your application through our careers page. Our process includes an initial conversation with the hiring manager, a case-based assessment focused on real-world HR leadership scenarios (no trick questions), and a team fit conversation. We move quickly and respect your time — expect a decision within two weeks of your final interview.

HR Manager

Location: Hybrid / Remote

About the Role

We're looking for an HR Manager to lead a small HR team (3-6 people) and grow into a confident people leader. You'll develop your team, manage delivery across HR cycles, and stay hands-on in your domain while building your management skills with support from senior leadership.

What You'll Do

  • Build and lead a high-performing HR team with clear ownership and psychological safety
  • Develop team members through effective 1:1s, feedback, and career development plans
  • Translate HR function goals into actionable team objectives
  • Maintain domain credibility and provide hands-on guidance when the team needs support
  • Manage delivery through performance reviews, compensation cycles, and compliance deadlines

What You Bring

  • 5-8 years of HR experience across one or more disciplines
  • Strong IC track record with recognition for quality and sound judgment
  • Informal leadership or mentorship experience
  • Working knowledge of employment law and compliance requirements
  • Clear communication skills across business leaders and HR stakeholders
  • Interest in people leadership beyond domain expertise

Nice to Have

  • HRIS and HR analytics experience
  • HR certifications (SHRM-CP, PHR)
  • Multi-discipline HR exposure
