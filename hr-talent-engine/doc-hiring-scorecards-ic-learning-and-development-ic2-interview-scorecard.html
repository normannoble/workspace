Level Context: What IC2 Looks Like

IC2 is a capable learning professional who independently designs and delivers standard training programs, creates e-learning content, and manages assigned program areas. Evaluate for:

  • Instructional design proficiency — Designs programs with clear learning objectives, appropriate activities, and logical content flow
  • Facilitation confidence — Leads workshops and training sessions with growing confidence and audience awareness
  • Stakeholder communication — Keeps stakeholders informed and gathers input that improves program relevance
  • Content management — Maintains organized, consistent, and up-to-date learning resources

Differentiation from IC1: IC2 independently designs and delivers programs; IC1 coordinates logistics and administration. Differentiation from IC3: IC2 designs and delivers assigned programs; IC3 conducts strategic needs analyses, measures business impact, and serves as a performance consultant.

Competency Assessment

1. Program Design & Delivery

What to assess: Ability to design training with solid instructional design principles, develop e-learning content, facilitate sessions, and maintain program currency.

IC2 Expectations: - Training programs designed with clear learning objectives, appropriate activities, and logical content flow - E-learning modules developed using authoring tools that are engaging, accessible, and aligned with learning standards - Workshops facilitated with growing confidence—managing group dynamics, staying on track, and adapting delivery - Program content maintained and updated based on evaluation feedback and changing business requirements

Sample Questions: - Walk me through a training program you designed from scratch. What was the learning need, how did you structure it, and what was the outcome? - Describe your experience creating e-learning content. What tools did you use and how did you ensure the content was engaging? - Tell me about a facilitation experience where you had to adapt in the moment. What happened and how did you handle it? - How do you decide when a program needs to be updated? Give me an example of how you kept content current.

2. Learning Evaluation & Reporting

What to assess: Ability to collect and analyze post-training evaluations, track participation data, connect training to behavior change, and iterate based on data.

IC2 Expectations: - Post-training evaluations consistently collected and analyzed with summary reports identifying what works and what needs adjustment - Completion and participation data tracked accurately with trends and anomalies flagged - Growing ability to connect training outcomes to behavior change beyond satisfaction surveys - Evaluation data used to make concrete improvements, creating a visible cycle of iteration

Sample Questions: - How do you evaluate whether a training program was effective? Walk me through your process. - Tell me about a time evaluation data revealed something surprising about a program. What did you find and what did you change? - How do you move beyond "smile sheets" to understand whether learning is being applied on the job? - Describe a specific improvement you made to a program based on participant feedback or evaluation data.

3. Stakeholder Communication & Needs Gathering

What to assess: Ability to keep stakeholders informed, gather meaningful input from managers and SMEs, distinguish stated requests from underlying gaps, and build productive relationships.

IC2 Expectations: - Program stakeholders kept informed about timelines, participation, and outcomes through clear communication - Information gathered from managers and SMEs that meaningfully improves program content and relevance - Growing skill in asking the right questions to understand what stakeholders actually need - Productive working relationships with facilitators, vendors, and cross-functional partners

Sample Questions: - Tell me about a time you worked with a subject matter expert to develop training content. How did you manage the collaboration? - Describe a situation where a stakeholder requested a specific training program but the real need was something different. How did you identify the gap? - How do you keep stakeholders informed about program progress and outcomes? Give me an example. - Tell me about a relationship you built with a cross-functional partner that made your L&D work more effective.

4. Learning Content & Resource Management

What to assess: Ability to maintain organized training materials, ensure consistent quality and branding, curate resources, and improve administrative processes.

IC2 Expectations: - A well-organized library of training materials, facilitator guides, and learner resources for assigned program areas - Content consistent in quality, branding, and instructional approach - Knowledge of available learning resources, tools, and external content for supplementing formal programs - Process improvements identified and implemented to reduce manual effort

Sample Questions: - How do you organize and maintain training materials across multiple programs? What system do you use? - Describe how you ensure consistency in quality and branding across the learning content you create. - Tell me about a time you recommended an external learning resource (course, tool, or content) to supplement a formal program. - Give me an example of a process improvement you made to the administrative side of your L&D work.

5. Professional Growth & Problem-Solving

What to assess: Problem-solving with common design and delivery issues, receptiveness to feedback, growth orientation, and initiative in learning new methodologies.

IC2 Expectations: - Problem-solving skills to diagnose common design and delivery issues and implement improvements - Receptiveness to feedback with demonstrated growth from facilitation observation and program reviews - Curiosity and initiative in learning new L&D methodologies, tools, and best practices - Reliable self-management, time estimation, and follow-through on program commitments

Sample Questions: - Tell me about a design or delivery problem you diagnosed and fixed. How did you identify the issue? - Describe a time you received feedback on your facilitation or program design. How did you incorporate it? - What L&D methodologies, tools, or trends have you learned about recently? How have you applied them? - How do you manage your time across multiple program commitments?
