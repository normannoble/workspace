30-60-90 Day Onboarding: Talent Acquisition Coordinator (IC1)

Overview

Level: IC1 — Talent Acquisition Coordinator Focus: Learning recruiting fundamentals, mastering coordination workflows, building operational reliability Manager's Role: Structured guide, active coach, regular feedback provider

IC1 TA Coordinators are early-career professionals building their recruiting foundation. Onboarding is highly structured with clear daily expectations, frequent check-ins, and progressive responsibility. The goal is to move from guided task execution to reliable independent coordination within 90 days, with a growing understanding of how each task connects to hiring outcomes.

Days 1-30: Learn

Theme: Build the Foundation

Primary Objectives: - Understand the recruiting lifecycle and how coordination supports each stage - Learn the ATS platform, scheduling tools, and communication templates - Build working relationships with recruiters, hiring managers, and interview panelists - Establish habits of accuracy, timeliness, and proactive communication

Week 1: Welcome & Orientation

Day Focus Activities
1 Welcome Team introductions, HR orientation, workspace and tool setup, overview of TA team structure and mission
2 Systems ATS walkthrough (navigation, candidate stages, data entry), calendar and scheduling tool setup, communication template review
3 Process Recruiting lifecycle overview—from job posting through offer—and how coordination supports each stage
4 Shadow Shadow a recruiter through a full day: intake call, sourcing, screens, scheduling, debrief
5 Practice Complete first guided scheduling task, review candidate communication templates, recap week with manager

Key Meetings to Schedule: - [ ] Manager 1:1 (weekly) - [ ] 1:1 with each TA team member - [ ] Introductions with key hiring managers - [ ] HR Operations team overview - [ ] IT/Facilities for tool access and workspace setup - [ ] Onboarding buddy check-in (if assigned)

Weeks 2-4: Learning the Workflows

Learning Goals: - [ ] Navigate the ATS confidently—update candidate stages, enter notes, run basic reports - [ ] Understand scheduling logistics including time zones, interviewer availability, and candidate preferences - [ ] Know the standard interview formats for common role types - [ ] Understand confidentiality requirements for candidate information - [ ] Recognize the key metrics the TA team tracks and why they matter

Activities: - Coordinate interview scheduling under recruiter guidance for 2-3 active requisitions - Practice writing candidate-facing communications using approved templates - Maintain ATS records for assigned requisitions with recruiter review - Attend recruiting team meetings and intake calls as an observer - Begin building a personal reference guide of processes and key contacts

Milestones: - [ ] First interview successfully scheduled end-to-end with minimal corrections - [ ] ATS data entry accurate on first attempt for routine transactions - [ ] Candidate communications sent on time with appropriate tone - [ ] Can explain the recruiting lifecycle stages and coordination touchpoints - [ ] Comfortable asking questions and knowing when to escalate

30-Day Check-in

Manager Discussion Points: - How comfortable are you with the ATS and scheduling tools? - What parts of the coordination workflow feel clear vs. confusing? - How are relationships developing with recruiters and hiring managers? - What additional support or training would be helpful? - Initial feedback on accuracy, timeliness, and communication quality

Success Indicators: - [ ] Can navigate the ATS and complete basic data entry independently - [ ] Understands the recruiting lifecycle and coordination responsibilities - [ ] Has scheduled interviews with decreasing need for correction - [ ] Communicates proactively about progress and blockers - [ ] Demonstrates curiosity about how coordination connects to hiring outcomes

Days 31-60: Contribute

Theme: Build Independence

Primary Objectives: - Manage scheduling and coordination for assigned requisitions with growing independence - Maintain ATS data hygiene standards consistently without reminders - Begin anticipating recruiter needs and identifying potential issues proactively - Contribute reliably to a smooth, timely hiring process

Weeks 5-6: Owning Coordination Tasks

Activities: - Take primary coordination responsibility for a defined set of active requisitions - Schedule interviews independently, confirming logistics and preparing interviewers - Maintain ATS records with consistent accuracy—stages updated, notes captured, data clean - Draft candidate communications for recruiter review, then send independently as confidence grows - Begin tracking personal accuracy and timeliness metrics

Demonstrating Reliable Coordination: - [ ] Interview scheduling completed accurately with logistics confirmed and all parties informed - [ ] ATS records maintained without reminders—stages current, notes clear, data hygiene met - [ ] Candidate communications sent on time with appropriate tone and content - [ ] Established processes followed consistently with understanding of the reasoning behind each step - [ ] Scheduling conflicts and process exceptions flagged before they become problems

Weeks 7-8: Growing Judgment

Activities: - Handle more complex scheduling scenarios (multi-panel interviews, cross-timezone coordination) - Start resolving routine scheduling conflicts and candidate questions independently - Contribute observations in team meetings about process bottlenecks or improvement ideas - Support recruiter with pre-interview logistics and post-interview feedback collection - Take on a stretch assignment (e.g., help organize a hiring event, update a process document)

Growing Impact: - [ ] Anticipating recruiter needs before being asked—preparing materials, flagging conflicts - [ ] Resolving routine issues independently while escalating appropriately when needed - [ ] Feedback from recruiters and hiring managers is positive on responsiveness and accuracy - [ ] Growing understanding of candidate experience and how coordination shapes it - [ ] Fewer repeat questions—building on prior learning consistently

60-Day Check-in

Manager Discussion Points: - How is your confidence managing coordination independently? - Where are you still needing guidance most? - What patterns or improvements have you noticed in the hiring process? - How are your working relationships with recruiters and hiring managers? - Feedback on accuracy, initiative, and professional growth trajectory

Success Indicators: - [ ] Managing coordination for assigned requisitions with minimal oversight - [ ] ATS data hygiene consistently meets team standards - [ ] Candidate communications are professional and timely - [ ] Proactively identifying scheduling conflicts and process gaps - [ ] Incorporating feedback effectively with visible improvement cycle-to-cycle

Days 61-90: Own

Theme: Establish Yourself

Primary Objectives: - Operate as a reliable, independent coordinator the team counts on - Demonstrate consistent accuracy, timeliness, and professional judgment - Show growing understanding of how coordination drives hiring outcomes - Establish habits and reputation that position you for continued growth

Weeks 9-10: Full Independent Coordination

Activities: - Manage full coordination workload for assigned requisitions independently - Handle complex scheduling scenarios and candidate communications confidently - Maintain ATS data accuracy and process compliance as a matter of habit - Support new team members or interns with basic process questions if applicable - Contribute process improvement suggestions based on hands-on experience

IC1 Expectations Met: - [ ] Scheduling completed accurately and efficiently across assigned requisitions - [ ] ATS records consistently accurate—data hygiene standards met without reminders - [ ] Candidate communications reflect the organization's commitment to a respectful process - [ ] Established processes followed with understanding, not just compliance - [ ] Positive contributions to team culture through reliability and follow-through

Weeks 11-12: Setting Long-Term Trajectory

Activities: - Discuss development goals and areas of interest within talent acquisition - Explore IC2 Recruiter path if interested—what skills to develop next - Document any process improvements or learnings to share with the team - Solidify relationships with recruiters and hiring managers - Fully transition from "new hire" mindset to established team contributor

Looking Ahead: - [ ] Clear understanding of your strengths and development areas in TA - [ ] Development plan established with manager - [ ] Relationships with recruiters and hiring managers solidified - [ ] Known as a dependable, detail-oriented coordinator - [ ] Positioned for continued growth toward IC2

90-Day Review

Manager Discussion Points: - Comprehensive onboarding assessment - Coordination quality and reliability evaluation - Professional growth and learning behaviors observed - Path forward—deepening coordination skills vs. expanding toward recruiting - Recognition and development areas

Success Indicators: - [ ] Operating reliably at IC1 level with consistent accuracy and timeliness - [ ] Coordination ownership clearly established for assigned requisitions - [ ] Growing understanding of recruiting beyond just coordination tasks - [ ] Strong working relationships with recruiters and hiring managers - [ ] Clear on continued growth path within talent acquisition

Key Relationships to Build

Who Why When to Meet
Direct manager Alignment, feedback, development Weekly
Assigned recruiters Day-to-day partnership, learning recruiting context Daily/as needed
Hiring managers Scheduling coordination, building trust As requisitions require
HR Operations team Understanding broader HR processes First month
Fellow coordinators Peer learning, coverage support Ongoing
IT support Tool access, troubleshooting First week, as needed

Common IC1 Onboarding Challenges

Challenge Solution
Overwhelmed by ATS and multiple tools Focus on one system at a time; build a personal cheat sheet; ask for repeated demos
Unsure when to ask vs. figure it out Default to asking early on; discuss the "ask vs. solve" threshold with manager
Scheduling mistakes due to time zones or conflicts Use a checklist for every scheduling task; double-check before sending
Feeling disconnected from recruiting outcomes Ask recruiters about candidate outcomes; attend debriefs when possible
Difficulty managing multiple requisitions simultaneously Use a tracking spreadsheet or task list; prioritize with recruiter input

Manager Checklist

Pre-Arrival

  • [ ] ATS access and training materials prepared
  • [ ] Scheduling tool and email accounts set up
  • [ ] Assigned requisitions identified for training
  • [ ] Onboarding buddy or mentor assigned
  • [ ] First two weeks of meetings scheduled

Week 1

  • [ ] Orientation and tool setup complete
  • [ ] ATS walkthrough and initial training done
  • [ ] Recruiter shadow day completed
  • [ ] First guided scheduling task assigned
  • [ ] Weekly 1:1 cadence established

Ongoing

  • [ ] Accuracy and timeliness tracking in place
  • [ ] Feedback delivered regularly with specific examples
  • [ ] Complexity of assignments increasing appropriately
  • [ ] Learning behaviors reinforced and encouraged
  • [ ] Course corrections made early when needed
