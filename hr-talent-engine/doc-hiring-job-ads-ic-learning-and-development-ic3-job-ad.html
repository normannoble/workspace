Senior L&D Specialist — Human Resources

The Opportunity

You'll design and deliver high-impact learning programs that measurably improve employee capability, performance, and career growth — and you'll do it by diagnosing the real problem first, not just building the training someone asked for. This is the role where deep instructional design expertise meets business impact: you'll conduct needs analyses that distinguish skill gaps from process issues, design programs across multiple modalities, and build evaluation frameworks that prove your work actually changes behavior. If you want to be a trusted advisor on capability development — not just a training catalog — and you have the design depth and facilitation mastery to deliver, this is your role.

What You'll Do

  • Build learning programs grounded in real needs — You'll diagnose performance gaps through needs analysis before prescribing solutions, distinguishing between skill gaps, process issues, and motivation problems. Your programs will solve the right problem because you validated the problem first.
  • Design experiences that change behavior, not just check boxes — You'll apply adult learning principles across instructor-led, virtual, self-paced, and blended modalities. Your portfolio will include leadership development, onboarding, manager programs, and capability-building that participants and their managers consistently cite as valuable.
  • Prove your programs work — You'll build evaluation frameworks that go beyond satisfaction scores to measure knowledge acquisition, behavior change, and business impact. Your stakeholder reports will communicate ROI in terms business leaders understand and act on.
  • Be the strategic partner for capability development — Business leaders and HRBPs will come to you when they need to solve performance challenges. You'll conduct performance consulting conversations that diagnose root causes and recommend the right intervention — which sometimes means telling stakeholders that training isn't the answer.
  • Build a learning culture, not just a course catalog — You'll create learning ecosystems that include formal programs, peer learning, knowledge repositories, and communities of practice. Your contribution extends beyond individual courses to how the organization approaches development as a continuous practice.

What You Bring

  • 4-7 years of professional L&D experience, including at least 2 years designing and delivering programs independently
  • Deep proficiency in instructional design methodologies (ADDIE, SAM, or equivalent) with strong command of adult learning theory
  • Strong facilitation and presentation skills — you lead engaging workshops for diverse audiences at multiple organizational levels
  • Experience with learning management systems, authoring tools (Articulate, Captivate, or equivalent), and digital learning platforms
  • Stakeholder management ability — you build trust with business leaders, conduct effective needs analyses, and push back when training isn't the right solution
  • Analytical skills for assessing performance gaps and evaluating program effectiveness with data
  • A track record of designing programs that resulted in measurable behavior change or performance improvement

Nice to Have

  • Experience with evaluation frameworks (Kirkpatrick or equivalent) and ROI measurement for learning programs
  • Background in performance consulting, organizational development, or change management
  • Mentorship experience developing junior L&D professionals' design and facilitation skills

Why [Company]

  • Solve real problems, not just build courses — We value L&D professionals who diagnose before they prescribe. You'll have the authority to push back when training isn't the answer and the support to design the right intervention when it is.
  • Your impact is measurable — We connect learning programs to business outcomes. You'll build evaluation frameworks that prove your work matters, and leadership will see the evidence.
  • Deep work, not content factories — You'll focus on high-impact programs that require real design thinking, not high-volume course production. Quality and impact over quantity.

How to Apply

Submit your application through our careers page. Our process includes a conversation with the L&D hiring manager, a portfolio review and design challenge, and a facilitation or presentation exercise. We aim to move quickly — expect a decision within two weeks of your final interview.
