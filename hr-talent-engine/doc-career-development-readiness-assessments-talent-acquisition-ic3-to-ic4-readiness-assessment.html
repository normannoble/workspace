Senior Recruiter to Principal Recruiter

How to Use This Assessment

This assessment evaluates readiness for Principal Recruiter (IC4). For each competency:

  • Employee self-assesses with specific examples of IC4-level work
  • Manager assesses based on observed behavior and impact
  • Discuss gaps and identify development actions

Rating Scale

Rating Description
1 - Not Yet Still operating at IC3 level; needs significant development
2 - Emerging Shows IC4 behaviors inconsistently; needs more practice
3 - Ready Consistently demonstrates IC4-level performance

Promotion readiness requires "3 - Ready" in most areas, with no areas at "1 - Not Yet."

1. Hiring Effectiveness

IC4 expectation: Solves the organization's most critical and complex hiring challenges with strategies others couldn't execute, and establishes assessment standards that raise the bar organization-wide.

1a. Complex Hiring Strategy

Does the employee develop and execute recruiting strategies for the organization's hardest hiring challenges—executive searches, niche technical roles, competitive-market talent—that others couldn't solve?
