Chief People Officer — Human Resources

The Opportunity

You'll serve as the company's senior-most people executive — a peer to the CEO, CFO, and CTO — with ultimate accountability for people strategy, organizational culture, talent capabilities, and the health of the employee experience. This is the role that defines how the company wins through its people over the next 3-5 years and beyond. You'll set the vision, build the executive bench, represent the company's people story to the board and investors, and steward a culture that attracts and retains exceptional talent at every level. If you've spent your career building toward leading an entire people function with full strategic authority, this is the opportunity.

What You'll Do

  • Set the people vision — You'll create and own a people strategy that builds sustainable competitive advantage through talent. Your bets on leadership development, culture, compensation philosophy, and organizational design will pay off over years and build cumulative advantage.
  • Lead as a C-suite peer — You'll operate as a true equal on the executive team, integrating people perspective into every major company decision — from strategy and M&A to product and market expansion. You'll present to the board on people strategy, succession planning, talent risk, and organizational health.
  • Build an HR function that's a competitive weapon — You'll lead and develop your HR leadership team (E1s, E2s, M3s), build succession plans at every senior level, and create a function known for attracting exceptional HR talent and delivering reliably.
  • Steward culture and talent — You'll deliberately shape a culture that is authentically lived and demonstrably connected to business performance. You'll ensure the company has the people it needs today and is building capability for the future.
  • Manage risk and drive governance — You'll identify and manage people risks across retention, compliance, organizational health, and culture. You'll ensure the organization responds effectively to sensitive situations, public scrutiny, and workforce disruptions.
  • Be the face of the employer brand — You'll represent the company's people story externally — to candidates, customers, partners, investors, and the industry. Your thought leadership will position the company as a destination for exceptional talent.

What You Bring

  • 18-25+ years of HR experience, with 10+ years in senior or executive leadership including significant time at Director or Senior Director level
  • Track record leading HR functions supporting 500+ employees with sustained business impact over multiple years
  • Experience developing executive-level HR leaders (E1s, E2s) who became effective senior people executives
  • Board-level communication experience — you've presented to boards, built board confidence in talent and organizational strategy, and handled difficult questions on people matters
  • M&A experience — people and culture due diligence, integration planning, and post-acquisition alignment
  • Broad and deep expertise spanning the full HR discipline — talent acquisition, learning & development, compensation & benefits, employee relations, people operations, organizational development, and DEI
  • C-level executive presence — you operate as a peer to the CEO, CFO, and CTO with full credibility and strategic influence

Nice to Have

  • Prior CPO, CHRO, or VP of People experience
  • Investor relations experience — contributed to fundraising narratives or public company communications with compelling people storytelling
  • Global employment expertise across multiple jurisdictions
  • Industry recognition as a thought leader on talent, culture, and the future of work

Why [Company]

  • Real authority at the top — This is a C-level role with genuine strategic authority, not a VP title with limited influence. You'll shape company direction from the people perspective and have a seat at every table that matters.
  • A company worth leading — We've built a strong foundation and are at a stage where the right CPO can define the next era of our people strategy. The business is growing, the challenges are real, and the opportunity to build something lasting is genuine.
  • A board that cares about people — Our board takes talent and culture seriously. You'll have an audience that values people strategy as a competitive lever, not just a risk to manage.

How to Apply

This is a confidential executive search. Please contact our executive recruiting team directly or reach out to the CEO's office. Our process is designed for C-level candidates — highly discreet, respectful of your current role, and structured to give you full visibility into the opportunity. Expect conversations with the CEO, board members, cross-functional C-suite peers, and the HR leadership team.
