Level Context: What IC2 Looks Like

IC2 is a solid talent acquisition professional who independently manages full-cycle recruiting for standard roles and is developing stronger hiring manager partnerships and sourcing capability. Evaluate for:

  • Full-cycle ownership — Manages requisitions independently from intake through offer
  • Sourcing fundamentals — Builds active pipelines through multiple channels with growing effectiveness
  • Stakeholder partnership — Provides reliable, consistent support to hiring managers and moves toward consultative partnership
  • Candidate experience — Maintains responsive, professional candidate communication throughout the process

Differentiation from IC1: IC2 owns full-cycle recruiting independently; IC1 supports recruiters through coordination. Differentiation from IC3: IC2 executes well on standard roles; IC3 handles complex/critical roles and elevates the function.

Competency Assessment

1. Full-Cycle Recruiting Execution

What to assess: Ability to independently manage requisitions from intake through offer close with consistent quality.

IC2 Expectations: - Requisitions filled with qualified candidates who meet role requirements, pass screening criteria, and are well-calibrated to hiring manager expectations - A managed requisition portfolio delivered within target timelines, with early communication when pipeline challenges require adjusted expectations - Offer acceptance rates that reflect strong candidate engagement and competitive positioning throughout the process - Screening and evaluation practices applied consistently, with growing sophistication in assessing candidate quality beyond surface qualifications

Sample Questions: - Walk me through how you manage a requisition from intake to offer. What are the key milestones? - Tell me about a role that was harder to fill than expected. What did you do to adjust? - How do you screen candidates for fit beyond what is on their resume? - Describe your approach to closing candidates. How do you handle competing offers?

2. Sourcing & Pipeline Development

What to assess: Effectiveness in building candidate pipelines through multiple channels and adapting sourcing strategies.

IC2 Expectations: - Active candidate pipelines maintained for each open requisition through inbound applications, direct sourcing, and referral generation - Sourcing techniques applied independently—including boolean search, LinkedIn outreach, and job board optimization—with growing effectiveness - A developing awareness of talent market conditions for core role types, informing realistic timeline expectations and sourcing adjustments - Candidate outreach that is personalized and compelling, generating response rates that meet or exceed team benchmarks

Sample Questions: - What sourcing channels do you use most frequently? How do you decide which to prioritize for a given role? - Walk me through how you would build a pipeline for a role you have never recruited for before. - Tell me about a time your initial sourcing approach was not working. What did you do differently? - How do you write outreach messages that get responses from passive candidates?

3. Hiring Manager Partnership

What to assess: Quality of communication, intake process execution, and growing consultative ability with hiring managers.

IC2 Expectations: - Hiring managers who receive consistent, reliable recruiting support—timely candidate submittals, clear pipeline status, and well-coordinated interview processes - Intake meetings conducted effectively, resulting in clear role requirements, aligned expectations, and realistic hiring timelines - Interview processes coordinated smoothly, with panels prepared, candidates informed, and feedback collected promptly - A growing ability to advise hiring managers on candidate quality, market conditions, and process improvements

Sample Questions: - How do you run an intake meeting with a hiring manager? What do you make sure to cover? - Describe a time a hiring manager had unrealistic expectations about a role. How did you handle it? - How do you keep hiring managers informed about pipeline progress? - Tell me about a time you provided advice to a hiring manager that changed how they approached a hire.

4. Candidate Experience & Employer Brand

What to assess: Responsiveness, professionalism, and care in candidate interactions throughout the hiring process.

IC2 Expectations: - A candidate experience that is responsive and organized at every stage—candidates know where they stand and what to expect next - Consistent, professional communication maintained throughout, including timely rejections delivered with respect - Employer value proposition communicated clearly and authentically during screens and outreach - A recruiting process that moves at a reasonable pace, with candidates not lost to unnecessary delays or communication gaps

Sample Questions: - What does a great candidate experience look like to you? How do you deliver that consistently? - How do you handle delivering a rejection to a candidate you have built a relationship with? - Tell me about a time a candidate had a poor experience in your process. What happened and what did you learn? - How do you communicate your organization's value proposition to candidates who have multiple options?

5. Professional Growth & Judgment

What to assess: Self-management, learning orientation, and developing recruiting judgment.

IC2 Expectations: - Reliable self-management, workload prioritization, and follow-through on commitments across multiple open requisitions - Problem-solving skills with ability to troubleshoot pipeline challenges and adjust sourcing strategies when initial approaches underperform - Receptiveness to feedback and demonstrated growth in recruiting judgment from one hiring cycle to the next - Curiosity and initiative in learning new sourcing tools, recruiting techniques, and talent market dynamics

Sample Questions: - How do you prioritize your time when managing multiple open requisitions with competing deadlines? - Tell me about a significant mistake you made in a recruiting process. What did you learn? - Describe how your recruiting approach has evolved over the past year. What prompted the changes? - How do you stay current with recruiting trends and tools?
