IC2 to IC3 Readiness Assessment

Junior HR Business Partner to HR Business Partner

How to Use This Assessment

This assessment evaluates readiness for HR Business Partner (IC3). For each competency:

  • Employee self-assesses with specific examples of IC3-level work
  • Manager assesses based on observed behavior and impact
  • Discuss gaps and identify development actions

Rating Scale

Rating Description
1 - Not Yet Still operating at IC2 level; needs significant development
2 - Emerging Shows IC3 behaviors inconsistently; needs more practice
3 - Ready Consistently demonstrates IC3-level performance

Promotion readiness requires "3 - Ready" in most areas, with no areas at "1 - Not Yet."

1. Strategic Business Partnership

IC3 expectation: Business leaders actively seek the HRBP's counsel on organizational decisions, people strategies are aligned with business objectives, and workforce planning anticipates rather than reacts to talent needs.

1a. Trusted Advisory Relationship

Do business leaders actively seek the employee's counsel on organizational decisions—including team structure, talent planning, succession, and change management—not just when problems arise?

2. Talent Management & Development

IC3 expectation: Drives effective talent review processes with executed development plans, builds succession plans for critical roles, and improves manager capability through coaching and intervention.

3. Employee Relations & Organizational Health

IC3 expectation: Handles employee relations issues with urgency, discretion, and fairness; monitors organizational health proactively; and manages difficult conversations with empathy and professionalism.

3a. Employee Relations Judgment
