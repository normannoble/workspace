Senior HR Business Partner — Human Resources

The Opportunity

You'll partner with VP and SVP-level executives on the most consequential organizational challenges — large-scale org design, M&A people integration, culture transformation, and enterprise talent strategy. This is the senior-most individual contributor HRBP role, where your judgment on organizational matters carries weight at the executive level and your interventions create lasting structural improvements to how the business manages talent. You won't just advise on people problems; you'll shape the organizational architecture, develop the leadership bench, and define what excellent business partnership looks like for other HRBPs. If you've built the strategic depth and executive influence to operate at this level — and you want to apply it to challenges worthy of your expertise — this is the role.

What You'll Do

  • Be the strategic advisor executives can't lead without — VP and SVP-level leaders will involve you in business planning, leadership team decisions, and strategic pivots. Your people strategies for major business segments will demonstrably contribute to business outcomes with clear, measurable connections between organizational interventions and performance improvements.
  • Drive organizational design at scale — You'll lead large-scale restructurings, M&A integration, and new business unit creation with minimal disruption and outcomes that hold up over time. Your workforce planning operates 12-24 months ahead, anticipating capability gaps and translating them into concrete talent investment decisions.
  • Develop the organization's most critical leaders — You'll build succession depth for key roles that's genuinely ready, coach senior leaders to measurably improve their effectiveness, and create talent mobility programs that break down silos and retain high-potential talent.
  • Handle the situations that require the most judgment — Executive misconduct investigations, systemic cultural issues, high-profile departures, and organizational crises will land on your desk. You'll navigate them with the discretion, strategic awareness, and composure they demand.
  • Shape enterprise HR strategy and develop other HRBPs — Your deep business insight will influence company-wide programs, and your mentorship will help IC2 and IC3 HRBPs develop into stronger business partners.

What You Bring

  • 8-12+ years of progressive HR experience, including at least 4 years in a strategic HRBP role supporting VP-level and above
  • Expert knowledge across HR disciplines with particular depth in organizational design, executive talent management, and change management
  • Sophisticated business acumen — you engage with senior executives on business strategy, financial performance, and competitive dynamics as a peer
  • Executive influence and advisory skills — you challenge, coach, and redirect VP/SVP-level leaders on sensitive people decisions while maintaining trust
  • Deep experience with organizational diagnostics at scale — you identify systemic dysfunction patterns and design multi-faceted interventions
  • Exceptional judgment under ambiguity and pressure — you make consequential decisions when information is incomplete and multiple interests are in tension
  • Resilience and composure in high-stakes situations — executive conflicts, sensitive investigations, and organizational crises

Nice to Have

  • M&A people integration experience — cultural assessment, integration planning, and retention strategy
  • Experience with large-scale culture transformation during periods of significant organizational change
  • HR certifications (SHRM-SCP, SPHR) or advanced degree (MBA, MS in I/O Psychology or OD)

Why [Company]

  • The highest-stakes organizational challenges — You'll work on org design, M&A integration, culture transformation, and executive talent strategy. The complexity here matches the expertise you've built.
  • Executive leaders who treat HR as a strategic function — Our senior leaders don't just tolerate HRBP involvement — they depend on it. You'll have genuine influence on the most consequential organizational decisions.
  • Legacy-level impact — Your organizational design work, leadership development programs, and cultural interventions will shape the company for years. This is work that outlasts any single business cycle.

How to Apply

Submit your application through our careers page, or reach out directly to our HR leadership team. Given the seniority of this role, our process is tailored — expect conversations with the CHRO or Head of HR, in-depth discussions about your approach to organizational challenges, and meetings with the executive leaders you'd partner with. We move efficiently and respect your time.
