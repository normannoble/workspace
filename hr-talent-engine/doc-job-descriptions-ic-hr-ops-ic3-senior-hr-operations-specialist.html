Senior HR Operations Specialist

Level: IC3 (Senior Individual Contributor)

Role Overview

The Senior HR Operations Specialist is a self-directed HR professional who owns critical HR systems, processes, and compliance programs, ensuring the operational backbone of HR runs reliably and scales with organizational growth. This role bridges execution with operational strategy—maintaining day-to-day HR infrastructure while driving process improvements that increase efficiency, accuracy, and employee experience.

Success in this role requires deep expertise in HRIS administration, employment compliance, and process design, combined with the ability to translate complex policy requirements into practical, scalable workflows. Senior HR Ops Specialists own significant operational areas, influence how HR delivers services across the organization, and serve as the go-to resource for systems, data, and compliance questions.

Success Outcomes

Success in this role means achieving the following outcomes:

HR Systems & Data Integrity

  • HRIS and HR technology platforms that are accurately configured, well-maintained, and trusted as the system of record for employee data across the organization
  • Employee data that is consistently accurate, complete, and audit-ready—with discrepancies identified and resolved proactively rather than discovered during audits or reports
  • System integrations between HRIS, payroll, benefits, and other platforms that function reliably, with data flowing correctly and exceptions handled promptly
  • Reporting and analytics capabilities that enable HR leadership and business partners to make data-informed decisions about workforce trends, headcount, and program effectiveness

Process Design & Operational Efficiency

  • HR processes (onboarding, offboarding, transfers, promotions, leave management) that are documented, standardized, and consistently executed—reducing errors and manual workarounds
  • Workflow improvements that measurably reduce cycle times, eliminate redundant steps, or improve the employee experience without compromising compliance requirements
  • Self-service capabilities and knowledge resources that empower employees and managers to resolve routine HR questions independently, reducing ticket volume and wait times
  • Operational playbooks and runbooks that enable consistent execution across the HR Ops team, reducing key-person dependencies

Compliance & Risk Management

  • Employment compliance programs (I-9, EEO, FLSA, ADA, state/local requirements) that are current, well-documented, and pass internal and external audits without material findings
  • Policy changes and regulatory updates that are identified, assessed for impact, and implemented across systems and processes before compliance deadlines
  • Employee records and documentation practices that meet legal retention requirements and can withstand scrutiny in the event of disputes or litigation
  • A proactive compliance posture—potential risks are identified and mitigated before they become violations or liabilities

Stakeholder Service & Collaboration

  • HR Business Partners and Centers of Excellence teams that view HR Ops as a reliable, responsive partner—not a bottleneck or black box
  • Employee inquiries and HR service requests that are resolved accurately and within SLA, with clear communication when issues require escalation or additional time
  • Cross-functional partnerships with IT, Finance, Legal, and Facilities that result in well-integrated processes and smooth handoffs for employee lifecycle events
  • A reputation as a trusted advisor on HR systems and processes whose input is sought when new programs, policies, or tools are being designed

Required Skills

HR Operations Expertise

  • Deep proficiency in HRIS administration (configuration, reporting, troubleshooting) with strong understanding of data architecture and system integrations
  • Strong knowledge of employment law and compliance requirements across federal, state, and local jurisdictions
  • Experience with HR process design, documentation, and continuous improvement methodologies
  • Working knowledge of payroll operations, benefits administration, and leave management as they intersect with HR systems

Leadership & Collaboration

  • Stakeholder management ability—can partner effectively with HRBPs, CoEs, IT, and business leaders to deliver operational solutions
  • Clear communication of complex policy, system, or process topics to both technical and non-technical audiences
  • Mentorship capability—can accelerate junior team members' growth through effective training, documentation, and knowledge sharing

Core Competencies

  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with ability to diagnose system issues, identify root causes, and implement sustainable fixes
  • Detail orientation combined with systems thinking—can manage precision work while understanding how individual processes fit into the broader HR operating model
  • Continuous learning orientation—stays current with HR technology trends, compliance changes, and operational best practices

Required Experience

Years of Experience

  • 4-7 years of professional HR operations experience, including at least 2 years in a senior or lead capacity

Demonstrated Track Record

  • Proven success managing HRIS platforms and ensuring data integrity at scale
  • History of designing or improving HR processes that resulted in measurable efficiency gains or error reduction
  • Experience managing compliance programs and successfully passing internal or external HR audits
  • Track record of cross-functional collaboration to implement system changes or new HR programs

Domain Experience

  • Hands-on administration experience with enterprise HRIS platforms (e.g., Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, ADP, BambooHR, or UKG)
  • Experience with HR analytics, reporting tools, and data visualization
  • Familiarity with ITSM or HR service delivery platforms (e.g., ServiceNow, Jira Service Management) for managing HR requests

Education

  • Bachelor's degree in Human Resources, Information Systems, Business Administration, or related field—or equivalent practical experience
  • HR certifications (PHR, SHRM-CP) are valued but not required
