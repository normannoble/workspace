How to Use This Assessment

This assessment evaluates readiness for Senior Recruiter (IC3). For each competency:

  • Employee self-assesses with specific examples of IC3-level work
  • Manager assesses based on observed behavior and impact
  • Discuss gaps and identify development actions

Rating Scale

Rating Description
1 - Not Yet Still operating at IC2 level; needs significant development
2 - Emerging Shows IC3 behaviors inconsistently; needs more practice
3 - Ready Consistently demonstrates IC3-level performance

Promotion readiness requires "3 - Ready" in most areas, with no areas at "1 - Not Yet."

1. Hiring Effectiveness

IC3 expectation: Fills critical roles with high-quality candidates who succeed long-term, drives structured evaluation practices, and consistently exceeds team benchmarks on offer acceptance.

1a. Critical Role Delivery

Does the employee successfully fill critical and complex roles with candidates who pass probation, receive strong early performance reviews, and remain beyond their first year?
