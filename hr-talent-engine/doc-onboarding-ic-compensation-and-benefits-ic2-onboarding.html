30-60-90 Day Onboarding: Compensation Analyst (IC2)

Overview

Level: IC2 — Compensation Analyst Focus: Building compensation program knowledge, mastering tools and data sources, establishing stakeholder relationships Manager's Role: Structured guidance, regular check-ins, progressive responsibility assignment

IC2 Compensation Analysts are independent contributors expected to handle core analysis work after an appropriate ramp period. Onboarding focuses on understanding the organization's compensation programs, mastering the data and tools, building relationships with HR partners, and beginning to deliver reliable analytical work. The goal is to move from learning the environment to independently managing compensation analyses and stakeholder requests within 90 days.

Days 1-30: Learn

Theme: Build the Foundation

Primary Objectives: - Understand the organization's compensation philosophy, salary structures, job architecture, and pay programs - Learn the HRIS, compensation tools, survey sources, and analytical workflows - Build relationships with compensation team members, HR Business Partners, and key stakeholders - Develop a clear picture of the annual compensation cycle, recurring deliverables, and current priorities

Week 1: Orientation & Context

Day Focus Activities
1 Welcome Team introductions, organizational overview, compensation function mission and priorities
2 Compensation Landscape Review salary structures, job architecture, pay ranges, and compensation philosophy documentation
3 Tools & Systems HRIS orientation, compensation management system access, survey platform walkthroughs
4 Data & Sources Overview of compensation surveys used, market data sources, benchmarking methodology
5 Stakeholders Meet HR Business Partners and recruiters who are primary compensation data consumers

Key Meetings to Schedule: - [ ] Manager 1:1 (weekly) - [ ] 1:1 with each compensation team member - [ ] HR Business Partners for key client groups - [ ] Recruiting team leads (offer benchmarking context) - [ ] HRIS/People Analytics team (data and systems context) - [ ] Previous analyst or predecessor for knowledge transfer

Weeks 2-4: Deep Immersion

Learning Goals: - [ ] Working proficiency with HRIS, compensation tools, and survey platforms - [ ] Understanding of job evaluation methodology and how roles are slotted into the framework - [ ] Familiarity with annual compensation cycle timeline, processes, and key deliverables - [ ] Knowledge of pay equity analysis approach and compliance requirements - [ ] Clear understanding of how compensation requests flow from stakeholders and how they're prioritized

Activities: - Shadow a senior analyst through a market pricing request end-to-end - Review recent compensation cycle materials—guidelines, manager communications, exception processes - Complete a supervised job evaluation to learn the organization's methodology - Review pay equity analysis documentation and methodology - Process 2-3 ad-hoc compensation requests with senior analyst guidance

Milestones: - [ ] Independently navigating HRIS and compensation systems for data retrieval - [ ] First supervised market pricing analysis completed - [ ] Can articulate the organization's salary structure, pay ranges, and job architecture - [ ] Understands the compensation request intake process and typical turnaround expectations - [ ] Relationships established with key HR Business Partners

30-Day Check-in

Manager Discussion Points: - How comfortable are you with the tools, data sources, and analytical workflows? - What aspects of the compensation programs are clear, and where do you need more context? - How are relationships with HR partners and the compensation team developing? - What support or resources would help you ramp faster? - Initial feedback on analytical quality and attention to detail

Success Indicators: - [ ] Can navigate HRIS, compensation tools, and survey platforms independently - [ ] Understands the organization's compensation framework and key programs - [ ] Has completed first supervised analytical deliverables - [ ] Key stakeholder relationships are established - [ ] Asking informed questions that show growing understanding

Days 31-60: Contribute

Theme: Build Independence

Primary Objectives: - Handle core compensation analyses (market pricing, job evaluations, benchmarking) with decreasing supervision - Respond to stakeholder compensation requests with accuracy and appropriate timeliness - Begin supporting compensation cycle preparation and recurring processes - Develop growing comfort with pay equity data and compliance documentation

Weeks 5-6: Establishing Capability

Activities: - Independently manage market pricing requests for standard roles - Conduct job evaluations using established methodology with peer review - Respond to HR partner and recruiter compensation inquiries - Begin contributing to survey participation submissions - Support compensation cycle preparation activities

Demonstrating Independence: - [ ] Market pricing analyses completed independently with reliable accuracy - [ ] Job evaluations consistently aligned with established methodology - [ ] HR partners receiving timely, helpful compensation guidance - [ ] Data integrity maintained in HRIS and compensation systems - [ ] Compensation documentation kept current and organized

Weeks 7-8: Growing Scope

Activities: - Handle more complex market pricing scenarios (hard-to-match roles, multi-source analysis) - Begin contributing to pay equity data preparation and analysis - Support benchmarking report preparation for leadership - Take ownership of specific compensation documentation maintenance - Process compensation exceptions and escalations with manager guidance

Growing Impact: - [ ] Handling a broader range of compensation requests without supervisor review - [ ] Contributing reliably to team deliverables and recurring processes - [ ] Pay equity data preparation demonstrating accuracy and methodology understanding - [ ] Stakeholders beginning to come directly to you for compensation questions - [ ] Quality of work consistently meeting team standards

60-Day Check-in

Manager Discussion Points: - How is the transition to independent analytical work progressing? - Where do you feel confident, and where do you still need support? - How are stakeholder relationships and responsiveness developing? - What recurring processes are you ready to own independently? - Feedback on accuracy, timeliness, and communication quality

Success Indicators: - [ ] Independently handling standard market pricing and job evaluation requests - [ ] HR partners receiving reliable, timely compensation guidance - [ ] Contributing to team deliverables and recurring processes - [ ] Data integrity maintained across compensation systems - [ ] Operating with growing independence and decreasing supervision

Days 61-90: Own

Theme: Establish Yourself

Primary Objectives: - Operate fully at IC2 level across core compensation responsibilities - Own specific recurring processes and analytical deliverables independently - Demonstrate reliable accuracy, timeliness, and stakeholder responsiveness - Establish trajectory for continued development

Weeks 9-10: Full IC2 Operation

Activities: - Manage full portfolio of compensation analysis requests independently - Own specific survey participation and benchmarking deliverables - Support annual compensation cycle processes with full contribution - Maintain pay equity documentation and compliance records - Contribute to team knowledge through documentation and process notes

IC2 Expectations Met: - [ ] Market pricing analyses delivered accurately and on schedule - [ ] Job evaluations completed reliably using established methodology - [ ] HR partners and recruiters receiving timely, accurate compensation guidance - [ ] Compensation documentation organized, accessible, and current - [ ] Data integrity maintained across HRIS and compensation systems

Weeks 11-12: Setting Long-Term Trajectory

Activities: - Identify areas where you can deepen expertise or improve processes - Set goals for next quarter aligned with team priorities - Discuss IC3 Senior Compensation Analyst path if interested - Solidify position and reputation with stakeholders - Fully transition from "new hire" mindset to established contributor

Looking Ahead: - [ ] Clear understanding of your ongoing responsibilities and deliverable ownership - [ ] Development plan established with manager - [ ] Relationships with HR partners and recruiters solidified - [ ] Known as a reliable, accurate compensation resource - [ ] Positioned for continued growth and deeper expertise

90-Day Review

Manager Discussion Points: - Comprehensive onboarding assessment - Analytical quality and accuracy evaluation - Stakeholder responsiveness and communication - Path forward (depth in specific comp areas vs. breadth) - Recognition and development areas

Success Indicators: - [ ] Operating fully at IC2 level - [ ] Analytical deliverables consistently accurate and on time - [ ] Stakeholder relationships productive and trusted - [ ] Strong foundational knowledge across compensation programs - [ ] Clear on continued growth path

Key Relationships to Build

Who Why When to Meet
Direct manager Alignment, feedback, development Weekly
Senior compensation analysts Mentorship, analytical guidance, knowledge transfer Weekly or biweekly
HR Business Partners Primary stakeholder relationship, comp advisory First month, ongoing
Recruiting team leads Offer benchmarking, market context First month, as needed
HRIS/People Analytics Data integrity, system support, reporting First month, as needed
Finance partners Budget context, compensation cost modeling First month, quarterly

Common IC2 Onboarding Challenges

Challenge Solution
Unfamiliar compensation survey tools or HRIS Request dedicated training sessions; practice with real data in sandbox environments
Organization's job architecture is complex or inconsistent Ask for the history and rationale; review past job evaluations for pattern understanding
Multiple stakeholders with competing urgency Establish prioritization framework with manager; communicate timelines proactively
Legacy data quality issues in compensation systems Document what you find; propose cleanup plans rather than working around bad data
Balancing accuracy with speed on ad-hoc requests Start with accuracy; speed develops naturally with familiarity. Never sacrifice correctness.

Ongoing
