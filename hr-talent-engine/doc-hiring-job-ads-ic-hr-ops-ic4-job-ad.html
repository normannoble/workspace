Principal HR Operations Specialist — Human Resources

The Opportunity

You'll define how the organization delivers HR services at scale — shaping HR technology strategy, designing the operating model, and driving the most complex system implementations and operational transformations that the function faces. This is a role for an HR operations leader who thinks in systems, not transactions: your influence won't be measured by tickets closed or processes maintained, but by the enterprise-wide architecture decisions, automation strategies, and compliance frameworks that you design and others adopt. If you've built the expertise to lead HRIS platform migrations, solve multi-jurisdiction compliance puzzles, and create operating models that scale with growth — and you want to apply it where it matters most — this is your role.

What You'll Do

  • Shape HR technology strategy for the organization — You'll define and drive the HR technology roadmap, lead major HRIS implementations and platform consolidations, and establish data architecture standards that ensure accuracy, consistency, and interoperability across all HR systems.
  • Design an operating model that scales — You'll evolve how HR delivers services as the organization grows — implementing automation that reduces manual effort, building end-to-end process optimization across the employee lifecycle, and establishing standards that are adopted broadly across the function.
  • Navigate the hardest compliance and regulatory challenges — You'll own multi-jurisdiction requirements, M&A integrations, and novel regulatory interpretations. Your compliance frameworks will prevent issues by design rather than relying on individual vigilance.
  • Influence across the organization — You'll partner with IT, Finance, Legal, and senior HR leadership on integrated solutions aligned with enterprise strategy. You'll communicate HR technology trade-offs to senior leadership in terms that enable informed investment decisions.
  • Build the next generation of HR Ops leaders — You'll mentor Senior HR Operations Specialists toward Principal-level capability, foster a culture of operational excellence, and establish the analytical standards and frameworks that elevate the entire function.

What You Bring

  • 8-12+ years of professional HR operations, HRIS, or HR technology experience
  • Expert-level depth in HR technology ecosystems — HRIS platforms, integrations, data architecture, and system design at enterprise scale
  • Track record leading major system implementations, platform migrations, or operating model transformations with organization-wide impact
  • The ability to influence HR technology direction and operational strategy across multiple teams without direct authority
  • Executive communication skills — you frame HR technology strategy and operational trade-offs for senior leadership audiences
  • Deep understanding of multi-jurisdiction compliance, regulatory frameworks, and the operational realities of large-scale HR environments
  • Systems thinking — you understand complex interdependencies across HR technology, processes, compliance, and employee experience

Nice to Have

  • Experience with M&A HR integration planning and execution
  • Background designing HR operations for multi-entity or multi-country environments
  • Track record building HR analytics capabilities and data-driven decision-making practices

Why [Company]

  • Enterprise-scale problems that require real architecture thinking — You'll design HR technology strategy and operating models for a growing organization, not just maintain what exists. The complexity here demands the expertise you've built.
  • Influence at the leadership level — Your technology roadmaps and operational strategies will be decision inputs for senior leadership. This is a role where HR operations expertise directly shapes investment priorities.
  • Build something that lasts — Your architecture decisions, compliance frameworks, and operating standards will define how HR operates for years. This is legacy-level work.

How to Apply

Submit your application through our careers page, or reach out directly to our HR leadership team. Given the seniority of this role, our process is tailored — expect conversations with the Head of HR Operations, a deep-dive on your approach to HR technology strategy and operational design, and discussions with cross-functional leaders you'd partner with. We move efficiently and respect your time.
