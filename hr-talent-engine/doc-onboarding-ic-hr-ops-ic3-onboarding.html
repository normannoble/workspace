30-60-90 Day Onboarding: Senior HR Operations Specialist (IC3)

Overview

Level: IC3 — Senior HR Operations Specialist Focus: Establishing systems and process ownership, driving operational improvements, building cross-functional partnerships Manager's Role: Context provider, strategic thought partner, appropriate autonomy

IC3 Senior HR Operations Specialists are experienced professionals expected to own critical HR systems, processes, and compliance programs. Onboarding focuses on quickly mapping the operational landscape, building credibility with stakeholders, and beginning to shape how the organization delivers HR services. The goal is to move from learning the environment to driving operational impact within 90 days.

Days 1-30: Learn

Theme: Map the Territory

Primary Objectives: - Deeply understand the HRIS architecture, system integrations, and data governance standards - Build relationships with HR partners, IT, Finance, Legal, and cross-functional stakeholders - Assess current process health, compliance posture, and operational efficiency gaps - Identify high-value opportunities where your expertise can have immediate impact

Week 1: Strategic Orientation

Day Focus Activities
1 Welcome Team introductions, organizational strategy, HR function structure, key priorities
2 HRIS Architecture System landscape, core platform configuration, integrations (payroll, benefits, time), data architecture
3 Process Landscape Employee lifecycle processes end-to-end, SOPs, service delivery model, known pain points
4 Compliance Compliance programs (I-9, EEO, FLSA, ADA), audit history, risk areas, regulatory calendar
5 Stakeholders Meet HRBPs, CoE leaders, IT partners, Finance—understand priorities, dependencies, and pain points

Key Meetings to Schedule: - [ ] Manager 1:1 (weekly) - [ ] 1:1 with each HR Ops team member - [ ] HR Business Partners for stakeholder needs and service quality feedback - [ ] IT/Systems team for HRIS architecture, integration health, and technology roadmap - [ ] Benefits, payroll, and compensation teams for process dependencies - [ ] Previous owner of your operational areas (if applicable)

Weeks 2-4: Deep Immersion

Learning Goals: - [ ] Expert-level understanding of the HRIS platform, configurations, and integration points - [ ] Clear view of compliance program health, audit readiness, and risk areas - [ ] Assessment of process efficiency—where manual workarounds, errors, or bottlenecks exist - [ ] Map of key stakeholders, their priorities, and how they experience HR operations - [ ] Initial assessment of where you can add the most value—systems, process, compliance, or service delivery

Activities: - Take ownership of a critical operational area or improvement initiative to demonstrate capability early - Audit HRIS data quality—run reports, identify discrepancies, assess data governance practices - Review compliance program documentation, audit findings, and regulatory change tracking - Assess HR service delivery model—ticket volume, resolution times, self-service adoption, pain points - Document findings and initial recommendations for manager discussion

Milestones: - [ ] Independently managing at least one critical operational area - [ ] Data quality assessment completed with initial findings documented - [ ] Can articulate the HR technology architecture and key integration dependencies - [ ] Have initial views on process improvements and compliance strengthening - [ ] Credibility established with key stakeholders through early engagement

30-Day Check-in

Manager Discussion Points: - What is your assessment of the operational landscape—systems, processes, compliance? - Where are the highest-value opportunities for improvement? - How are stakeholder relationships developing? - What resources or context do you need to accelerate? - Initial feedback on operational expertise and partnership quality

Success Indicators: - [ ] Clear mental model of the HRIS landscape and operational priorities - [ ] Key stakeholder relationships established - [ ] First contributions demonstrating IC3 operational capability - [ ] Identified 2-3 high-value improvement opportunities - [ ] Operating with appropriate ownership and strategic perspective

Days 31-60: Contribute

Theme: Drive Impact

Primary Objectives: - Own critical HR systems, processes, and compliance programs with strong results - Drive process improvements that increase efficiency, accuracy, or employee experience - Begin mentoring junior HR Ops team members and building team capability - Establish yourself as the go-to resource for systems, data, and compliance questions

Weeks 5-6: Establishing Ownership

Activities: - Take full ownership of assigned HRIS areas—configuration, reporting, troubleshooting, optimization - Drive a process improvement initiative that addresses a known pain point or inefficiency - Ensure compliance programs are current, documented, and audit-ready for your areas - Build reporting and analytics capabilities that enable data-informed HR decisions - Begin coaching junior team members on systems, processes, and operational best practices

Demonstrating Ownership: - [ ] HRIS platforms accurately configured, well-maintained, and trusted as system of record - [ ] Employee data consistently accurate, complete, and audit-ready - [ ] System integrations functioning reliably with exceptions handled promptly - [ ] Compliance programs current and documented for your areas - [ ] Stakeholders view you as the go-to resource for systems and process questions

Weeks 7-8: Expanding Influence

Activities: - Contribute to cross-functional process improvements beyond immediate operational area - Share operational insights and data to inform HR leadership decisions - Drive self-service improvements or knowledge resources that empower employees and managers - Build operational playbooks and runbooks that reduce key-person dependencies - Represent HR Ops expertise in cross-functional projects and system change discussions

Growing Impact: - [ ] Process improvements delivering measurable efficiency gains or error reduction - [ ] Self-service capabilities or knowledge resources reducing ticket volume - [ ] Operational playbooks documented for consistent team execution - [ ] Junior team members seeking your guidance on complex situations - [ ] Recognized as a trusted advisor on HR systems and processes

60-Day Check-in

Manager Discussion Points: - How is ownership of critical operational areas feeling? - What impact have your process improvements had so far? - Where do you want to expand influence across HR operations? - How are mentoring relationships with junior team members? - Feedback on operational leadership and stakeholder partnership

Success Indicators: - [ ] Full ownership of critical systems and processes established - [ ] Process improvements driving measurable results - [ ] Mentoring and knowledge sharing visible across the team - [ ] Stakeholder relationships built on operational credibility - [ ] Contributing at IC3 level consistently

Days 61-90: Own

Theme: Solidify as Senior HR Operations Specialist

Primary Objectives: - Operate fully at IC3 level across all operational responsibilities - Shape how the organization delivers HR services in your areas - Demonstrate sustained impact on operational quality and team capability - Establish trajectory for continued growth and influence

Weeks 9-10: Full IC3 Operation

Activities: - Own outcomes completely for assigned systems, processes, and compliance programs - Drive improvements that measurably reduce cycle times, errors, or manual workarounds - Lead compliance program management—regulatory updates identified and implemented proactively - Mentor effectively—junior team members growing through your coaching and documentation - Contribute to HR technology discussions and system roadmap planning

IC3 Expectations Met: - [ ] HRIS platforms trusted as system of record with data consistently accurate and audit-ready - [ ] HR processes documented, standardized, and consistently executed with reduced errors - [ ] Workflow improvements measurably reducing cycle times and manual effort - [ ] Compliance programs current, documented, and passing audits without material findings - [ ] HR partners and stakeholders viewing HR Ops as a reliable, responsive partner

Weeks 11-12: Setting Long-Term Trajectory

Activities: - Document operational vision and improvement roadmap for your areas - Set goals for next quarter aligned with HR Ops function priorities - Discuss IC4 Principal HR Operations Specialist path if interested - Solidify position and reputation across the HR function - Fully transition from "new hire" mindset to established senior contributor

Looking Ahead: - [ ] Clear vision for operational excellence in your areas - [ ] Development plan established with manager - [ ] Relationships with HR partners and cross-functional stakeholders solidified - [ ] Known as the go-to senior HR Ops specialist for your domains - [ ] Positioned for continued impact and influence

90-Day Review

Manager Discussion Points: - Comprehensive onboarding assessment - Operational ownership and improvement impact evaluation - Influence and operational leadership observed - Path forward (IC3 depth vs. IC4 strategic breadth) - Recognition and growth areas

Success Indicators: - [ ] Operating fully at IC3 level - [ ] Ownership of critical systems and processes clearly established - [ ] Operational influence demonstrated through improvements and mentoring - [ ] Strong stakeholder relationships and reputation - [ ] Clear on continued growth path

Key Relationships to Build

Who Why When to Meet
Direct manager Alignment, feedback, growth Weekly
HR Business Partners Stakeholder needs, service quality, cross-functional coordination Weekly or biweekly
IT/Systems team HRIS support, integration management, technology roadmap First month, ongoing
Junior HR Ops team members Mentoring, knowledge sharing, team capability building Ongoing
Benefits, payroll, compensation teams Process dependencies, data integration First month, ongoing
Finance and Legal contacts Compliance, audit support, regulatory guidance First month, as needed

Common IC3 Onboarding Challenges

Challenge Solution
HRIS architecture more complex than expected Prioritize understanding critical integrations; build system maps; leverage IT partnership
Not given enough ownership of critical systems early Advocate for scope; demonstrate readiness through quick wins; document your assessment
Compliance landscape varies from prior organization Map regulatory requirements systematically; build compliance calendar; consult Legal early
Existing processes deeply embedded despite inefficiency Build credibility first; propose improvements with data; respect institutional knowledge
Mentoring in a new environment where trust is still forming Start with documentation and knowledge sharing; build relationships through reliability
