Senior Director of HR

The Opportunity

You'll operate across the full breadth of HR — ensuring every domain works together to deliver a coherent employee experience and genuine competitive advantage through people. As a close partner to the Chief People Officer, you'll help shape enterprise people strategy, develop Directors into future executives, and drive organizational transformation at company scale. This isn't domain leadership — it's general management of the people function. If you're ready to make HR the reason a company wins, not just a function it has, this is the role.

What You'll Do

  • Lead across the full HR portfolio — You'll ensure Talent Acquisition, HR Operations, HRBP, L&D, and Compensation & Benefits work together to deliver a seamless, coherent employee experience. You'll optimize for company outcomes, not domain outcomes, resolving cross-domain challenges with lasting solutions.
  • Partner at the executive level — You'll serve as a close partner to the CPO, contribute to enterprise people strategy, and represent the HR function credibly in executive forums and board contexts. Your relationships with the CFO, CTO, and business unit leaders will drive company-level outcomes.
  • Develop executive-caliber HR leaders — You'll grow Directors (E1s) toward Senior Director and CPO-level capability, build succession plans at the executive level, and create an HR leadership culture that models excellence for the broader organization.
  • Drive organizational transformation — You'll position HR as a driver of change — leading cultural shifts, organizational redesigns, and workforce transformations that deliver lasting results. People practices will evolve ahead of business needs, not lag behind them.
  • Shape the company's external people story — You'll represent the company's people capabilities to candidates, customers, partners, investors, and industry. Your thought leadership will build employer brand and attract exceptional talent to the function and the company.

What You Bring

  • 15-20+ years of HR experience, with 8-12+ years in senior leadership including meaningful time at the Director level or equivalent
  • Track record leading HR organizations of significant scale supporting 500+ employees across multiple domains
  • Experience developing Director-level HR leaders who became effective executives
  • Strategic thinking with 2-5 year horizons — you shape company direction through people strategy
  • Board-level communication capability — you represent people strategy, talent metrics, and organizational health credibly to boards and investors
  • Cross-functional executive partnership at the C-suite level driving company-level outcomes
  • Experience leading major organizational transformations — significant growth, cultural change, M&A integration, or restructuring

Nice to Have

  • Experience deputizing for or closely partnering with a CPO or CHRO
  • Understanding of global employment practices and regulatory complexity across jurisdictions
  • Industry recognition as a people and culture leader — speaking, writing, or community involvement

Why [Company]

  • Enterprise-level impact — You'll shape how the entire HR function operates and how the company wins through its people. This is general management of a strategic function, not a bigger version of domain leadership.
  • A real partnership with the CPO — This role isn't about executing someone else's strategy. You'll co-create enterprise people strategy and have the authority and trust to drive it across the organization.
  • The right moment — We're at an inflection point where the right senior HR leader can build systems, culture, and leadership that define the company for years. The foundation is strong; we need someone who can take it to the next level.

How to Apply

We prefer to start with a confidential conversation. Reach out directly to our executive recruiting team or the CPO's office. Our process is designed for senior executives — discreet, efficient, and respectful of your current commitments. Expect conversations with the CPO, CEO, cross-functional C-suite partners, and members of the HR leadership team.
