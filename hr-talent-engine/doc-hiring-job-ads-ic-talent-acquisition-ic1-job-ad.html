Talent Acquisition Coordinator — Human Resources

The Opportunity

You'll launch your recruiting career at the center of how we build teams — coordinating interviews, supporting recruiters, and ensuring every candidate has a seamless experience from first contact to offer. This is a role where you'll learn the full recruiting lifecycle from the inside out, with real mentorship and a clear path to becoming a recruiter yourself. If you're organized, curious about talent acquisition, and energized by fast-paced work that directly shapes who joins the company, this is your starting point.

What You'll Do

  • Keep the hiring engine running smoothly — You'll own interview scheduling end-to-end: coordinating across time zones, preparing interviewers, and making sure candidates always know what to expect. When logistics go right, great hires happen faster.
  • Be the candidate's first impression — Your communications will set the tone for every candidate's experience. You'll ensure every touchpoint — from confirmation emails to follow-ups — reflects a company that respects people's time and treats hiring seriously.
  • Build the data foundation recruiters rely on — You'll maintain our ATS with accurate, up-to-date candidate records so the team can make decisions based on clean data, not guesswork. Your attention to detail keeps the whole pipeline honest.
  • Grow into a recruiting professional — You'll learn how sourcing, screening, and hiring decisions connect by working alongside experienced recruiters who invest in your development. Every hiring cycle will deepen your judgment and expand your capabilities.

What You Bring

  • Strong organizational skills and the ability to juggle multiple scheduling requests without dropping details
  • Clear, professional written communication — you'll be drafting candidate-facing emails daily
  • Comfort with scheduling tools, calendars, and productivity software (spreadsheets, email, collaboration platforms)
  • Attention to detail and genuine care for getting logistics right the first time
  • Curiosity about talent acquisition and a desire to build a career in recruiting
  • A collaborative, reliable approach — the team counts on you to follow through

Nice to Have

  • Previous experience with an applicant tracking system (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, or similar)
  • Internship or work experience in recruiting, HR, or administrative coordination
  • Exposure to fast-paced environments where multitasking and prioritization were essential

Why [Company]

  • Learn from people who invest in your growth — You'll work with experienced recruiters who see developing you as part of their job, not an afterthought. Structured mentorship and real feedback, not just observation.
  • Your work has visible impact — Every interview you coordinate and every candidate you communicate with directly affects who joins the company. You'll see the results of your effort in real time.
  • A launchpad, not a dead end — We've built a clear development path from coordinator to recruiter. Your growth here is planned, not left to chance.

How to Apply

Submit your application through our careers page. Our process includes an introductory conversation with the recruiting team, a practical exercise focused on coordination and communication skills, and a team culture conversation. We move quickly and keep you informed at every step — expect a decision within two weeks of your final interview.
