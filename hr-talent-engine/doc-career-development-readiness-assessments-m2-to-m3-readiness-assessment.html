M2 to M3 Readiness Assessment

Senior HR Manager to Head of HR

Field Value
Candidate
Sponsor
Date
Time at M2
Current Scope

Assessment Context

M3 (Head of HR) is a senior leadership role requiring demonstrated ability to develop other HR managers, shape HR organizational capability at scale, and contribute to company strategy. This assessment is typically conducted with input from executive leadership.

  • Candidate prepares evidence packet documenting M3-level impact
  • Sponsor presents case to assessment panel
  • Panel evaluates evidence against M3 criteria
  • Executive input is incorporated given the role's scope

Rating Scale

Rating Description
1 - Not Yet Operating at strong M2 level; organizational impact not yet demonstrated
2 - Emerging Shows M3 impact in some areas; needs broader demonstration or more time
3 - Ready Consistently demonstrates M3-level impact and capability

M3 readiness requires "3 - Ready" in most areas. This is a high bar for senior leadership.

1. Developing Other HR Managers

M3 expectation: Primary work is developing M1 and M2 HR managers into excellent people leaders; delegates meaningfully; builds a management culture within HR that reflects the same rigor the function asks of the rest of the organization.

1a. Manager Development Impact

Have HR managers this person developed become excellent leaders themselves? Is there clear evidence of accelerated growth in management capability—not just HR domain competence?

3. Strategic Leadership
