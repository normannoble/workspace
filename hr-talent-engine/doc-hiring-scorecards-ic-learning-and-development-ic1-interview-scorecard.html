Level Context: What IC1 Looks Like

IC1 is an early-career learning professional focused on building foundational skills in training operations, program logistics, and learning administration. Evaluate for:

  • Operational reliability — Ensures training sessions run smoothly with all logistics handled
  • Administrative accuracy — Maintains accurate learner records, compliance tracking, and materials management
  • Growth orientation — Demonstrates curiosity, asks thoughtful questions, and shows increasing capability over time
  • Professional foundation — Clear communication, organizational skills, and follow-through

Differentiation from IC2: IC1 focuses on coordination and logistics; IC2 independently designs and delivers training programs.

Competency Assessment

1. Training Logistics & Coordination

What to assess: Ability to organize training sessions, manage scheduling, coordinate with vendors and facilitators, and build reliable logistics processes.

IC1 Expectations: - Training sessions well-organized with all logistics handled—room bookings, materials prepared, technology tested, facilitators briefed - Scheduling that accounts for participant availability, facilitator calendars, and business cycles - Vendor and facilitator coordination that is timely and professional - A reliable process for managing logistics that requires less oversight over time

Sample Questions: - Tell me about a time you coordinated a multi-stakeholder event or session. How did you keep track of all the moving pieces? - Describe a situation where something went wrong with logistics at the last minute. How did you handle it? - How do you approach scheduling when you need to coordinate across many different people's calendars? - Give me an example of a process you created or improved to make coordination tasks more efficient.

2. LMS Administration & Compliance Tracking

What to assess: Ability to maintain accurate learner records, track compliance training, manage course catalogs, and provide LMS support.

IC1 Expectations: - Learner records accurate, up to date, and audit-ready - Compliance training tracked with clear visibility into completion rates, deadlines, and at-risk employees - Course catalog and content library maintained in an organized state - LMS support provided that resolves common issues and escalates technical problems appropriately

3. Program Support & Materials Management

What to assess: Ability to prepare training materials accurately, manage participant communications, collect evaluations, and build process documentation.

IC1 Expectations: - Training materials prepared accurately and delivered on time for each program - Participant communications sent consistently and clearly, contributing to a professional learner experience - Post-training evaluations collected and compiled into summary reports - A growing library of templates, checklists, and process documentation that makes recurring tasks more efficient

Sample Questions: - Tell me about a time you prepared materials or communications for a group event or program. How did you ensure quality and timeliness? - How do you approach writing professional communications (emails, invitations, reminders) for a large audience? - Describe your experience collecting and compiling feedback or survey data. How did you organize the results? - Give me an example of a template, checklist, or document you created to make a recurring task more efficient.

4. Learning & Professional Development

What to assess: Curiosity about adult learning, growing familiarity with L&D tools and methodologies, receptiveness to feedback, and initiative in building new capabilities.

IC1 Expectations: - Developing understanding of adult learning principles and instructional design concepts - Growing familiarity with L&D tools, technologies, and methodologies through observation, participation, and self-directed learning - Feedback incorporated effectively, with visible improvement over time - Initiative demonstrated in identifying process improvements and volunteering for stretch assignments

5. Communication & Reliability

What to assess: Written and verbal communication quality, organizational skills, adaptability, and attention to detail.

IC1 Expectations: - Clear written and verbal communication—professional participant communications and effective coordination with facilitators and vendors - Organizational skills to manage multiple concurrent training events and deadlines - Adaptability when plans change—training schedules shift, technology fails, or participant needs evolve - Reliability and follow-through on commitments—the team can count on tasks being completed accurately and on time
