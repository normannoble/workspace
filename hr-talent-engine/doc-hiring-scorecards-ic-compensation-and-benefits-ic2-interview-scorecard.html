Level Context: What IC2 Looks Like

IC2 is an independent contributor handling core compensation analysis including market pricing, salary structures, job evaluations, and annual comp cycle support. Evaluate for:

  • Analytical rigor — Produces accurate, reliable compensation analyses with minimal rework
  • Compensation fundamentals — Solid foundation in market pricing, job evaluation, and salary structure concepts
  • Stakeholder responsiveness — Delivers timely, clear compensation guidance to HR partners and recruiters
  • Process reliability — Manages recurring comp processes (annual cycle, surveys, documentation) dependably

Differentiation from IC1: IC2 works independently on core analyses; IC1 requires significant guidance and handles more routine tasks. Differentiation from IC3: IC2 executes reliably on established processes; IC3 owns programs strategically and advises business leaders.

Competency Assessment

1. Compensation Program Design & Maintenance

What to assess: Ability to maintain salary structures, conduct job evaluations, and support annual compensation cycles with accuracy and reliability.

IC2 Expectations: - Salary structures and pay ranges that are current, well-documented, and consistently applied - Job evaluations completed accurately and on time using established methodology - Annual compensation cycle processes executed reliably with clean data and minimal errors - Compensation program documentation that is organized, accessible, and kept up to date

Sample Questions: - Walk me through how you conduct a job evaluation. What methodology have you used and how do you ensure consistency? - Describe your role in the most recent annual compensation cycle you supported. What was your contribution and what went well or poorly? - Tell me about a time you identified a data quality issue in compensation systems. How did you find it and what did you do? - How do you ensure salary structure documentation stays current and accessible to HR partners?

2. Market Analysis & Benchmarking

What to assess: Proficiency with compensation survey data, market pricing methodology, and ability to produce reliable benchmarking analyses.

IC2 Expectations: - Market pricing analyses that use appropriate survey sources, match jobs accurately, and produce reliable competitive positioning data - Benchmarking reports delivered on schedule that clearly communicate market trends and competitive gaps - Survey participation managed effectively with accurate, on-time submissions - A growing understanding of compensation trends that strengthens analysis quality over time

Sample Questions: - How do you approach market pricing a new role? Walk me through your process from receiving the request to delivering the analysis. - What compensation surveys have you worked with? How do you decide which sources to use for a given role? - Tell me about a situation where market data conflicted across sources. How did you reconcile the data and reach a recommendation? - How do you communicate benchmarking results to someone without a compensation background?

3. Pay Equity & Compliance

What to assess: Understanding of pay equity concepts, compliance awareness, and ability to maintain data integrity for audit-ready documentation.

IC2 Expectations: - Pay equity analyses conducted with accurate data and sound methodology, surfacing potential disparities for review - Compensation practices that comply with applicable wage and hour laws, pay transparency requirements, and internal policies - Audit-ready documentation maintained for all compensation decisions, adjustments, and exceptions - Data integrity in HRIS and compensation systems that supports reliable reporting and analysis

Sample Questions: - Describe your experience with pay equity analysis. What methodology have you used and what was the outcome? - How do you stay current with compensation-related regulations and compliance requirements? - Tell me about a time you discovered a data integrity issue that could have affected pay equity reporting. What did you do? - How do you ensure that compensation decisions are documented in a way that would hold up under audit?

4. Stakeholder Advisory & Communication

What to assess: Ability to provide clear, timely compensation guidance and communicate data-driven recommendations to non-technical stakeholders.

IC2 Expectations: - HR Business Partners and recruiters who receive timely, accurate compensation guidance for offers and pay decisions - Compensation recommendations supported by clear data and rationale in accessible formats - Responsive turnaround on ad-hoc requests without sacrificing accuracy - A reputation as a reliable, knowledgeable resource for compensation questions

How did you handle it? - How do you present a compensation recommendation to a hiring manager who disagrees with the data? - Describe a situation where you had to explain a complex compensation concept to a non-technical audience. 5. Analytical & Technical Skills

What to assess: Excel/data proficiency, HRIS experience, attention to detail, and problem-solving in analytical work.

IC2 Expectations: - Strong Excel/spreadsheet skills including advanced functions, pivot tables, and data analysis - Comfort with HRIS and compensation management systems - Attention to detail and data accuracy in all analytical work - Problem-solving orientation that identifies discrepancies and investigates root causes
