Level Context: What IC2 Looks Like

IC2 is a solid operational contributor who independently manages HR processes end-to-end across assigned areas with consistent quality and growing judgment. Evaluate for:

  • HRIS proficiency — Administers system transactions accurately and maintains data quality through audits and reconciliation
  • Process ownership — Manages employee lifecycle processes end-to-end with minimal rework
  • Compliance awareness — Completes compliance reporting accurately and identifies potential gaps proactively
  • Growing independence — Handles routine and moderately complex work independently while knowing when to escalate

Differentiation from IC1: IC2 manages processes end-to-end independently; IC1 completes well-defined tasks with guidance. Differentiation from IC3: IC2 executes reliably across assigned areas; IC3 owns critical systems and drives process improvement at scale.

Competency Assessment

1. HR Systems & Data Integrity

What to assess: Proficiency in HRIS administration, data quality management, and reporting capability.

IC2 Expectations: - HRIS administered effectively for assigned areas, with transactions processed accurately, completely, and within established SLAs - Data quality maintained through regular audits, reconciliation activities, and timely correction of identified discrepancies - Reports and data extracts produced reliably for stakeholders, with a growing understanding of how HR data supports business decisions - System configurations and workflows maintained within assigned areas, with changes tested and documented appropriately

Sample Questions: - Walk me through how you ensure data accuracy when processing a batch of employee transactions in an HRIS. - Tell me about a data discrepancy you identified during an audit or routine task. How did you diagnose and resolve it? - Describe your experience generating HR reports. How do you validate the data before sharing with stakeholders? - How do you approach testing a system configuration change before deploying it?

2. Process Execution & Operational Efficiency

What to assess: Ability to manage employee lifecycle processes end-to-end, identify improvement opportunities, and maintain SOPs.

IC2 Expectations: - Employee lifecycle processes (onboarding, transfers, promotions, offboarding) managed end-to-end with consistent quality and minimal rework - Opportunities to streamline repetitive tasks or eliminate manual steps identified and raised, with proposed solutions where possible - Standard operating procedures maintained and improved based on practical experience administering the processes - Self-directed task management and reliable execution across multiple concurrent workflows and deadlines

Sample Questions: - Walk me through how you manage the onboarding or offboarding process from start to finish. What steps do you own? - Tell me about a time you identified an opportunity to streamline or improve a repetitive HR process. 3. Compliance & Risk Management

What to assess: Understanding of employment regulations, compliance reporting accuracy, and ability to identify potential risks.

IC2 Expectations: - Compliance reporting (EEO, benefits filings, audit requests) completed accurately and on schedule for assigned areas - Employment records and documentation maintained in accordance with federal, state, and local regulatory requirements - Potential compliance gaps or risks identified during routine work and escalated with relevant context before they become issues - A solid working knowledge of key employment regulations and how they translate into operational requirements

Sample Questions: - Describe your experience with compliance reporting. What types of reports have you prepared and what was your process for ensuring accuracy? - Tell me about a time you identified a potential compliance risk or gap. What did you do? - How do you stay current with employment regulations that affect HR operations? - What steps do you take to ensure employee records meet legal retention and documentation requirements?

4. Stakeholder Service & Collaboration

What to assess: Quality of HR inquiry resolution, cross-functional partnership, and emerging support of junior team members.

IC2 Expectations: - Escalated HR inquiries from employees and managers resolved thoroughly, with clear communication and appropriate follow-up - Effective partnerships with HR Centers of Excellence (benefits, talent acquisition, compensation) to coordinate cross-functional processes - Process handoffs and dependencies managed proactively, ensuring smooth end-to-end experiences for employees and managers - Beginning to support more junior team members with process guidance and knowledge sharing

What steps did you take to ensure a thorough resolution? - Describe a cross-functional process you coordinated with another HR team (benefits, compensation, talent acquisition). What made it work? - How do you manage handoffs between different teams to ensure nothing falls through the cracks? - Have you had the opportunity to help a more junior team member learn a process? 5. Problem-Solving & Professional Growth

What to assess: Diagnostic ability for process issues, receptiveness to feedback, and initiative in developing new skills.

IC2 Expectations: - Problem-solving skills with ability to diagnose process breakdowns and identify root causes in moderately complex situations - Receptiveness to feedback and demonstrated growth in accuracy, efficiency, and process knowledge - Initiative in learning new system capabilities, regulations, and operational best practices - Good judgment about priorities and trade-offs when managing competing deadlines
