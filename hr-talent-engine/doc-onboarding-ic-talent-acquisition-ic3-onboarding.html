30-60-90 Day Onboarding: Senior Recruiter (IC3)

Overview

Level: IC3 — Senior Recruiter Focus: Establishing hiring ownership, influencing recruiting practices, building strategic stakeholder partnerships Manager's Role: Context provider, strategic thought partner, appropriate autonomy

IC3 Senior Recruiters are experienced professionals expected to own hiring outcomes and influence recruiting direction. Onboarding focuses on quickly mapping the talent landscape, building credibility with hiring managers, and beginning to shape how the organization attracts and evaluates talent. The goal is to move from learning the environment to driving impact on critical hiring within 90 days.

Days 1-30: Learn

Theme: Map the Territory

Primary Objectives: - Deeply understand the organization's talent strategy, hiring priorities, and recruiting operations - Build relationships with hiring managers, TA leadership, and cross-functional partners - Assess current pipeline health, process effectiveness, and sourcing strategy gaps - Identify high-value opportunities where your expertise can have immediate impact

Week 1: Strategic Orientation

Day Focus Activities
1 Welcome Team introductions, organizational strategy, hiring priorities, TA function mission and goals
2 Talent Landscape Review open requisitions, hiring velocity, pipeline health, critical and hard-to-fill roles
3 Process & Tools ATS workflow, sourcing tool stack, structured interview methodology, evaluation criteria standards
4 Stakeholders Meet key hiring managers, understand their teams, pain points, and hiring history
5 Market & Brand Employer brand positioning, talent market intelligence for key segments, competitive landscape

Key Meetings to Schedule: - [ ] Manager 1:1 (weekly) - [ ] 1:1 with each recruiting team member - [ ] Senior hiring managers for critical requisitions - [ ] TA leadership for function strategy and priorities - [ ] HR Business Partners for workforce planning context - [ ] Compensation and total rewards team

Weeks 2-4: Deep Immersion

Learning Goals: - [ ] Expert-level understanding of the ATS, sourcing tools, and recruiting analytics - [ ] Clear view of hiring challenges, pipeline bottlenecks, and process strengths/weaknesses - [ ] Map of key stakeholders, decision-makers, and influence dynamics - [ ] Understanding of structured interviewing standards and how consistently they are applied - [ ] Initial assessment of where you can add the most value—critical roles, process gaps, team development

Activities: - Take ownership of a critical or complex requisition to demonstrate capability early - Review recent hiring outcomes—quality of hire, time-to-fill, offer acceptance rates - Assess interview panel calibration and evaluation consistency across teams - Identify sourcing strategy gaps for recurring or hard-to-fill role types - Document findings and initial recommendations for manager discussion

Milestones: - [ ] Independently managing at least one critical requisition - [ ] First qualified candidate submitted that meets hiring manager calibration - [ ] Can articulate the organization's talent challenges and competitive positioning - [ ] Have initial views on process improvements and sourcing strategy enhancements - [ ] Relationships with key hiring managers established with credibility

30-Day Check-in

Manager Discussion Points: - What is your assessment of the TA function's effectiveness and gaps? - What opportunities for impact have you identified? - How are hiring manager relationships developing? - What context or resources do you need to accelerate? - Initial feedback on recruiting quality and stakeholder partnership

Success Indicators: - [ ] Clear mental model of the talent landscape and hiring priorities - [ ] Key hiring manager relationships established - [ ] First contributions demonstrating IC3 recruiting capability - [ ] Identified 2-3 high-value opportunities for improvement - [ ] Operating with appropriate ownership and strategic thinking

Days 31-60: Contribute

Theme: Drive Impact

Primary Objectives: - Own end-to-end hiring for critical roles with strong results - Influence recruiting practices and stakeholder partnerships - Begin mentoring junior recruiters and elevating team capability - Establish yourself as a trusted talent advisor, not just an order-taker

Weeks 5-6: Establishing Ownership

Activities: - Manage full portfolio of critical and complex requisitions - Drive intake processes that result in calibrated requirements and realistic timelines - Build and maintain diverse candidate pipelines through proactive sourcing strategies - Share talent market intelligence with hiring managers to inform hiring decisions - Begin coaching junior recruiters on sourcing, screening, or stakeholder management

Demonstrating Ownership: - [ ] Hiring managers view you as a strategic partner—seeking input on role design and interview strategy - [ ] Sourcing strategies tailored to role type and market conditions - [ ] Structured evaluation methods applied consistently across your interview panels - [ ] Proactive communication about pipeline health, market challenges, and adjusted expectations - [ ] Quality bar maintained—candidates who meet calibration and are well-assessed

Weeks 7-8: Expanding Influence

Activities: - Contribute to team-wide recruiting practice improvements - Share talent market intelligence that informs workforce planning discussions - Train or calibrate interview panels for quality and consistency - Build candidate relationships that extend beyond immediate requisitions - Represent your expertise in cross-functional discussions on hiring strategy

Growing Impact: - [ ] Recruiting influence extending beyond personal requisitions - [ ] Junior recruiters seeking your guidance on complex situations - [ ] Contributing to team standards—interview calibration, sourcing playbooks, process improvements - [ ] Voice in recruiting direction and practice decisions - [ ] Recognized expertise in talent markets for assigned areas

60-Day Check-in

Manager Discussion Points: - How is ownership of critical hiring feeling? - What hiring outcomes have you driven so far? - Where do you want to expand influence across the TA function? - How are mentoring relationships with junior recruiters developing? - Feedback on strategic partnership and recruiting leadership

Success Indicators: - [ ] Full ownership of critical requisitions with strong pipeline quality - [ ] Hiring managers treating you as a trusted advisor - [ ] Mentoring and multiplying effect visible on the team - [ ] Stakeholder relationships built on credibility and expertise - [ ] Contributing at IC3 level consistently

Days 61-90: Own

Theme: Solidify as Senior Recruiter

Primary Objectives: - Operate fully at IC3 level across all recruiting responsibilities - Shape how the organization attracts and evaluates talent for your areas - Demonstrate sustained impact on hiring quality and team capability - Establish trajectory for continued growth and influence

Weeks 9-10: Full IC3 Operation

Activities: - Own hiring outcomes completely for critical and complex roles - Drive process improvements that improve hiring quality or efficiency - Mentor effectively—junior recruiters growing through your coaching - Maintain candidate relationships that reduce future sourcing effort - Contribute to recruiting strategy discussions with informed perspectives

IC3 Expectations Met: - [ ] Critical roles filled with high-quality candidates who pass probation and stay - [ ] Offer acceptance rates consistently exceeding team benchmarks - [ ] Interview panels calibrated and consistently applying structured evaluation - [ ] Feedback loops with hiring managers driving continuous improvement - [ ] Candidate experience that is responsive, transparent, and generates positive impressions

Weeks 11-12: Setting Long-Term Trajectory

Activities: - Document sourcing strategies and market intelligence for your areas - Set goals for next quarter aligned with TA function priorities - Discuss IC4 Principal Recruiter path if interested - Solidify position and reputation across the organization - Fully transition from "new hire" mindset to established senior contributor

Looking Ahead: - [ ] Clear vision for talent strategy in your areas - [ ] Development plan established with manager - [ ] Relationships with hiring managers and leadership solidified - [ ] Known as a go-to senior recruiter and trusted talent advisor - [ ] Positioned for continued impact and influence

90-Day Review

Manager Discussion Points: - Comprehensive onboarding assessment - Hiring effectiveness and quality evaluation - Influence and recruiting leadership observed - Path forward (IC3 depth vs. IC4 strategic breadth) - Recognition and growth areas

Success Indicators: - [ ] Operating fully at IC3 level - [ ] Hiring ownership clearly established for critical roles - [ ] Influence on recruiting practices demonstrated - [ ] Strong stakeholder relationships and reputation - [ ] Clear on continued growth path

Key Relationships to Build

Who Why When to Meet
Direct manager Alignment, feedback, growth Weekly
Senior hiring managers Strategic partnership, calibration, trust Weekly or biweekly
TA leadership Function strategy, priorities, influence First month, ongoing
Junior recruiters Mentoring, team capability building Ongoing
HR Business Partners Workforce planning, department context First month, quarterly
Compensation team Offer strategy, competitive positioning First month, as needed

Common IC3 Onboarding Challenges

Challenge Solution
Not given critical enough requisitions early Advocate for scope; demonstrate expertise quickly on initial assignments
Hiring managers resistant to advisory partnership Build credibility through delivery first; share market data; earn trust incrementally
Unfamiliar talent markets for new industries or roles Research deeply; leverage team knowledge; connect with external networks
Stepping on established recruiter relationships Navigate carefully; understand history; focus on adding value, not displacing
Mentoring not natural in a new environment Start with knowledge sharing; be available for questions; build relationships first

Manager Checklist

Pre-Arrival

  • [ ] Critical requisition portfolio identified
  • [ ] Key hiring manager introductions scheduled
  • [ ] Sourcing tools and ATS access provisioned
  • [ ] Initial project or high-impact requisition scoped
  • [ ] Team expectations for IC3 contribution set

Week 1

  • [ ] Strategic orientation complete—talent landscape understood
  • [ ] Hiring manager introductions done
  • [ ] ATS, sourcing tools, and process context transferred
  • [ ] First critical requisition kicked off
  • [ ] Regular cadence established

Ongoing

  • [ ] Ownership of critical hiring progressing
  • [ ] Influence opportunities provided across the team
  • [ ] Mentoring relationships forming with junior recruiters
  • [ ] Recruiting leadership developing
  • [ ] Course corrections made as needed
