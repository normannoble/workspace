Principal Recruiter — Human Resources

The Opportunity

You'll shape how the organization acquires its most critical talent — leading executive searches, solving the hardest hiring challenges before they become blockers, and defining the recruiting strategies that others execute. This is a role for a recruiting leader who operates at the intersection of deep expertise and strategic influence: your work won't be measured by individual requisitions filled, but by the lasting impact you have on how the company attracts, evaluates, and secures the talent that drives its future. If you've built the judgment to handle executive search, M&A talent integration, and workforce planning for capabilities that don't yet exist — and you want to use it where it matters — this is your role.

What You'll Do

  • Solve the talent challenges no one else can — You'll own the organization's most complex hiring: executive searches, niche technical roles, competitive-market talent, and positions that define new organizational capabilities. Your strategies will become the playbooks others follow.
  • Build talent pipelines for the future, not just today — You'll proactively develop relationships with senior and specialized talent, map markets before requisitions open, and ensure the organization is never caught flat-footed for strategic hires.
  • Advise senior leaders on talent strategy — You'll be a strategic partner to executives on organizational design, succession planning, and leadership team composition. Your market intelligence will inform workforce planning and compensation decisions at the leadership level.
  • Elevate the entire TA function — You'll influence recruiting practices across the team through expertise and credibility, mentor Senior Recruiters into future leaders, and establish assessment standards that raise the bar on hiring quality organization-wide.
  • Position the company as a destination for top talent — Your personal network, industry presence, and candidate relationship management will strengthen the organization's reputation in the talent markets that matter most.

What You Bring

  • 8-12+ years of professional recruiting experience, including significant time leading executive and senior-level searches
  • Expert command of executive search methodology, talent mapping, and competitive intelligence
  • Strategic fluency with talent market dynamics, compensation benchmarking, and workforce planning
  • The ability to influence recruiting practices and talent strategy across the function without direct authority
  • Executive communication skills — you frame talent strategy and trade-offs for C-suite and board-level audiences
  • Deep understanding of employment law, executive compensation structures, and compliance requirements for senior-level hiring
  • A systems thinker who understands how talent decisions ripple across the organization

Nice to Have

  • Experience with M&A talent due diligence and integration planning
  • Track record building recruiting strategies for entirely new organizational capabilities or market entry
  • Background in both retained search and in-house recruiting environments

Why [Company]

  • The highest-stakes talent decisions — You'll work on executive search, strategic workforce planning, and the talent challenges that shape the organization's trajectory. This is recruiting at its most consequential.
  • Your influence is structural, not just personal — We want a Principal Recruiter whose impact lives in the playbooks, standards, and strategies the function adopts — not just in the individual hires you close.
  • Senior leaders who value talent expertise — Our executives actively seek input on organizational design and leadership composition. You won't have to fight for a seat at the table.

How to Apply

Submit your application through our careers page, or reach out directly to our talent team. Given the seniority of this role, our process is tailored — expect a conversation with the Head of TA, a deep-dive on your approach to strategic recruiting challenges, and discussions with senior leaders you'd partner with. We move efficiently and respect your time.
