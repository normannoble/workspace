HR Business Partner — Human Resources

The Opportunity

You'll be a strategic advisor embedded within business units, partnering with leaders to translate organizational goals into people strategies that drive performance, engagement, and growth. This is the role where deep HR expertise meets business acumen — you'll diagnose organizational health issues, design practical interventions, manage complex employee relations, and own the people strategy for your client groups. Business leaders won't just consult you when problems arise; they'll involve you in organizational decisions because your judgment on talent matters adds real value. If you want to be the connective tissue between the business and HR — with the trust and influence to shape how teams are built and led — this is your role.

What You'll Do

  • Be the strategic advisor leaders actually rely on — Business leaders in your client groups will actively seek your counsel on team structure, talent planning, succession, and change management. You'll develop people strategies that are clearly aligned with business objectives and translate into measurable outcomes.
  • Build organizational talent from the inside — You'll run talent reviews that accurately identify high performers and at-risk employees, build succession plans for critical roles with genuinely ready successors, and coach managers to develop stronger people leadership capabilities over time.
  • Handle the hardest people situations with judgment and skill — You'll manage complex employee relations with the right balance of urgency, discretion, fairness, and legal awareness. You'll monitor organizational health through engagement data, turnover trends, and skip-level conversations — surfacing issues before they become crises.
  • Make enterprise HR programs work in the real world — You'll deploy performance reviews, compensation cycles, and engagement initiatives within your business units with high participation and quality, while channeling business-specific insights back to HR Centers of Excellence to ensure programs reflect on-the-ground realities.
  • Drive change that sticks — Your change management approach accounts for business unit context, timing, and leader readiness — resulting in genuine adoption rather than compliance theater.

What You Bring

  • 5-8 years of progressive HR experience, including at least 2 years in a business partner or strategic HR advisory capacity
  • Deep knowledge of HR disciplines — talent management, organizational development, employee relations, compensation, and employment law
  • Strong business acumen — you understand business models, financial drivers, and competitive dynamics well enough to connect people strategies to business outcomes
  • Executive presence and influence skills — you advise, challenge, and coach leaders without positional authority
  • Experience diagnosing organizational health issues and designing evidence-based interventions
  • Exceptional judgment in ambiguous, sensitive situations — you balance employee advocacy, business needs, legal risk, and organizational culture
  • Systems thinking — you understand how decisions in one area cascade into others

Nice to Have

  • Experience with organizational design, restructuring, or change management at meaningful scale
  • Working knowledge of change management frameworks and practical application experience
  • HR certifications (SHRM-CP/SCP, PHR/SPHR) or related credentials

Why [Company]

  • Strategic partnership, not program administration — Our business leaders treat their HRBP as an indispensable advisor on organizational decisions. You'll have real influence on how teams are structured, talent is developed, and change is managed.
  • Complex problems that require real judgment — The employee relations cases, organizational health challenges, and talent decisions here aren't formulaic. You'll exercise professional judgment on situations that matter.
  • Your impact is measurable — We connect people strategies to business outcomes. You'll see the results of your organizational interventions in engagement, retention, performance, and team effectiveness data.

How to Apply

Submit your application through our careers page. Our process includes a conversation with the Head of HR Business Partners, a scenario-based discussion on organizational challenges and employee relations judgment, and meetings with business leaders you'd partner with. We aim to move quickly and respect your time — expect a decision within two weeks of your final interview.
