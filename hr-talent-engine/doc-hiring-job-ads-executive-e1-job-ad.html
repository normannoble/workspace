Director of HR

The Opportunity

You'll own a major HR domain — such as Talent & Development, Employee Experience & Operations, or Total Rewards & People Analytics — with full accountability for how that domain drives business performance, organizational health, and competitive advantage through people. This is an executive role where you set strategic direction with a 1-3 year horizon, develop Heads of HR (M3s) into future executives, and partner with fellow senior leaders to shape company strategy. If you're ready to move beyond managing HR programs to owning the outcomes those programs create, this is the role.

What You'll Do

  • Own a domain that creates business value — You'll lead an HR domain with full accountability for outcomes: program effectiveness, adoption, employee experience, and measurable business impact. Your strategic vision will anticipate organizational needs 1-3 years ahead, positioning the company to attract, develop, and retain the talent it needs.
  • Lead as an executive — You'll contribute to company strategy as a member of the HR leadership team, build cross-functional partnerships with Finance, Technology, Operations, and other functions, and make strategic trade-offs with company-level perspective.
  • Develop senior HR leaders — You'll grow M3s toward Director-level capability through deliberate development and increasing strategic exposure. You'll build a leadership bench that ensures continuity and creates growth opportunities across the HR function.
  • Scale programs that compound — You'll design people investments that create leverage — platforms, frameworks, and capabilities that multiply effectiveness across the entire organization, not just within your domain.
  • Build organizational health at scale — You'll ensure high engagement and retention, maintain an inclusive culture that drives better outcomes, and achieve sustainable delivery without burning out your team.

What You Bring

  • 12-18+ years of HR experience, with 6-10+ years in management including experience at the Head of HR level or equivalent
  • Track record leading HR organizations or domains supporting 200+ employees with sustained business impact
  • Experience developing M2/M3-level HR leaders who became effective senior managers
  • Deep functional expertise in at least one major HR domain (talent acquisition, learning & development, compensation & benefits, employee relations, people operations, or organizational development)
  • Strategic thinking with 1-3 year horizons — you connect people decisions to business outcomes and competitive positioning
  • Executive presence and communication effectiveness with C-level, board, and external audiences
  • Business acumen — you understand revenue, costs, competitive dynamics, and how people strategy creates organizational value

Nice to Have

  • Experience with board-level communication or investor discussions on people metrics
  • Track record navigating M&A integration, rapid scaling, or cultural transformation from a people perspective
  • Experience designing people programs that scaled across geographies or business units

Why [Company]

  • Executive impact, not executive theater — You'll have genuine ownership over your domain with the authority to set strategic direction and the accountability that comes with it. Your outcomes will be visible to the business.
  • A seat at the real table — Our HR leadership team participates in company strategy. You'll shape how the company invests in people, not just execute programs designed by others.
  • A team that's ready — You'll work alongside experienced HR leaders and inherit strong M3s worth developing. The foundation is built; we need an executive who can take the domain to the next level.

How to Apply

For a role at this level, we prefer a direct conversation. Reach out to our recruiting team or submit your application through our careers page. Our process includes conversations with the CPO, cross-functional executive partners, and your future leadership team. We handle executive hiring with confidentiality and respect for your time.
