Senior HR Operations Specialist — Human Resources

The Opportunity

You'll own the critical HR systems, processes, and compliance programs that form the operational backbone of the organization — ensuring HR infrastructure runs reliably, scales with growth, and enables data-driven decisions across the company. This isn't maintenance work; you'll drive process improvements that measurably increase efficiency, design workflows that eliminate manual workarounds, and serve as the go-to expert on HRIS configuration, data integrity, and employment compliance. If you want to shape how HR operates — not just keep it running — and you bring the systems depth and process design skills to back it up, this is your role.

What You'll Do

  • Make HR systems the trusted source of truth — You'll own HRIS configuration, system integrations, and data governance so that employee data is consistently accurate, audit-ready, and flows reliably between payroll, benefits, and other platforms. When leadership needs workforce analytics, your systems deliver.
  • Design processes that scale — You'll standardize and improve HR processes across onboarding, offboarding, transfers, and leave management — reducing errors, eliminating redundant steps, and building self-service capabilities that empower employees and managers to resolve routine questions independently.
  • Own compliance so the organization can sleep at night — You'll manage employment compliance programs (I-9, EEO, FLSA, ADA, state/local requirements) proactively — identifying risks before they become violations and ensuring audit readiness is a continuous state, not a scramble.
  • Be the advisor other HR teams rely on — You'll partner with HRBPs, CoEs, IT, Finance, and Legal as a trusted resource on HR systems and processes. When new programs or policies are being designed, your input on operational feasibility and systems impact will be sought.
  • Develop the next generation of HR Ops professionals — You'll mentor junior team members, create operational playbooks that reduce key-person dependencies, and build knowledge-sharing practices that make the whole team stronger.

What You Bring

  • 4-7 years of professional HR operations experience, including at least 2 years in a senior or lead capacity
  • Deep proficiency in HRIS administration — configuration, reporting, troubleshooting, and understanding of data architecture and system integrations
  • Strong knowledge of employment law and compliance requirements across federal, state, and local jurisdictions
  • Experience with HR process design, documentation, and continuous improvement
  • Stakeholder management ability — you partner effectively with HRBPs, IT, and business leaders to deliver operational solutions
  • Analytical and problem-solving skills for diagnosing system issues, identifying root causes, and implementing sustainable fixes
  • An ownership mentality — you take accountability for operational outcomes, not just individual transactions

Nice to Have

  • Experience with ITSM or HR service delivery platforms (ServiceNow, Jira Service Management, or similar)
  • HR certifications (PHR, SHRM-CP) or related credentials
  • Background in HR analytics, reporting tools, and data visualization

Why [Company]

  • Systems depth meets strategic impact — You'll work at the intersection of technical HR systems expertise and organizational process design. Your improvements will be measured in efficiency gains and error reduction, not just uptime.
  • Compliance that's proactive, not reactive — We build compliance into our operating model rather than treating it as an afterthought. You'll lead that approach, not fight for it.
  • Your expertise shapes how HR delivers — We value HR Ops professionals who think in systems and solve structural problems. Your input on new programs and tools isn't optional — it's expected.

How to Apply

Submit your application through our careers page. Our process includes a conversation with the HR Ops hiring manager, a deep-dive on your approach to systems administration and process design, and a cross-functional team conversation. We move efficiently — expect a decision within two weeks of your final interview.
