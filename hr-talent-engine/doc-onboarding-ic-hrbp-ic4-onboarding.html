30-60-90 Day Onboarding: Senior HR Business Partner (IC4)

Overview

Level: IC4 — Senior HR Business Partner Focus: Assessing organizational landscape, building executive trust, shaping people strategy for major business segments Manager's Role: Strategic thought partner, executive sponsor, organizational navigator

IC4 Senior HRBPs are expert-level professionals who partner with VP and SVP-level executives on the most complex organizational challenges. Onboarding at this level is less about learning HR fundamentals and more about rapidly building executive trust, understanding organizational power dynamics, and identifying where strategic people interventions will create the most lasting impact. The goal is to become an indispensable advisor to senior leadership within 90 days.

Days 1-30: Learn

Theme: Assess the Landscape

Primary Objectives: - Develop deep understanding of supported business segments' strategy, competitive position, and leadership dynamics - Build relationships with VP/SVP-level leaders and understand their priorities, challenges, and decision-making styles - Assess organizational health, talent pipeline strength, and cultural dynamics at a systemic level - Understand the HR operating model, enterprise strategy, and where Senior HRBP influence is most needed

Week 1: Executive Orientation

Day Focus Activities
1 Welcome HR leadership intros, enterprise strategy overview, organizational context, stakeholder map
2 Business Strategy Deep-dive on supported business segments—P&L, growth plans, competitive dynamics, strategic pivots
3 Leadership & Talent Executive team profiles, succession pipeline, talent review outcomes, leadership effectiveness data
4 Organizational Health Engagement trends, attrition patterns, cultural dynamics, active ER situations, pending org changes
5 Executive Intros One-on-ones with VP/SVP leaders in supported segments, HR leadership, and peer Senior HRBPs

Key Meetings to Schedule: - [ ] Manager 1:1 (weekly) - [ ] 1:1 with each VP/SVP in supported business segments - [ ] CHRO or HR VP (alignment on strategic priorities) - [ ] Peer Senior HRBPs for cross-segment context - [ ] Legal counsel for employment law landscape and active cases - [ ] Previous Senior HRBP (if applicable) for thorough handoff

Weeks 2-4: Strategic Assessment

Learning Goals: - [ ] Expert-level understanding of business segment strategies, financial health, and competitive positioning - [ ] Assessment of leadership team effectiveness, succession depth, and executive development needs - [ ] Systemic view of organizational health—patterns across teams, leaders, and business cycles - [ ] Understanding of active and anticipated organizational changes (restructurings, M&A, growth initiatives) - [ ] Clear view of where strategic people interventions will create the most business impact

Activities: - Conduct confidential conversations with senior leaders to understand leadership dynamics and organizational tensions - Review succession plans, talent pipeline data, and leadership assessment results for critical roles - Analyze multi-year engagement, attrition, and organizational health trends across supported segments - Assess pending or likely organizational design challenges (scale, M&A integration, restructuring) - Document strategic assessment with initial recommendations for people strategy priorities

Milestones: - [ ] Can engage credibly with VP/SVP leaders on their business strategy and organizational challenges - [ ] Leadership pipeline and succession depth assessed for critical roles - [ ] Organizational health patterns identified across supported segments - [ ] Initial strategic people priorities forming based on business need and impact potential - [ ] Executive relationships initiated with early trust signals established

30-Day Check-in

Manager Discussion Points: - What is your strategic assessment of the supported business segments? - What are the highest-impact people strategy opportunities you see? - How are executive relationships developing? - What organizational dynamics or risks should we be aware of? - Initial feedback on executive presence and strategic impact

Success Indicators: - [ ] Deep strategic understanding of supported business segments - [ ] Executive relationships initiated with VP/SVP leaders - [ ] Systemic organizational health assessment completed - [ ] 2-3 high-impact strategic priorities identified - [ ] Operating with senior-level judgment and initiative

Days 31-60: Contribute

Theme: Shape Direction

Primary Objectives: - Begin influencing strategic people decisions at the executive level - Own the people strategy for supported business segments - Address the most complex organizational and talent challenges - Demonstrate the strategic advisory value that defines IC4 partnership

Weeks 5-6: Establishing Strategic Influence

Activities: - Lead strategic workforce planning conversations with VP/SVP leaders - Drive organizational design recommendations for team structure, accountability, and scaling - Coach senior leaders on their effectiveness as people leaders - Own the most complex and sensitive ER situations (executive-level, systemic cultural issues, high-stakes decisions) - Begin shaping talent strategy—succession planning, high-potential acceleration, critical role pipeline

Demonstrating Executive Advisory: - [ ] VP/SVP leaders actively involving Senior HRBP in business planning and strategic discussions - [ ] Organizational design recommendations producing improved structure and accountability - [ ] Executive coaching relationships forming with demonstrable value - [ ] Complex ER situations handled with judgment that balances all stakeholder interests - [ ] Talent strategy taking shape with concrete succession and development plans

Weeks 7-8: Driving Enterprise Impact

Activities: - Influence enterprise HR strategy through business-grounded insight and advocacy - Lead or champion large-scale organizational initiatives (restructuring, culture transformation, M&A integration) - Mentor IC2 and IC3 HRBPs on business partnership quality and strategic thinking - Build cross-functional coalitions with Finance, Legal, and executive leadership on people initiatives - Establish systematic feedback loops between business reality and HR Centers of Excellence

Growing Impact: - [ ] Enterprise HR strategy influenced through deep business insight - [ ] Large-scale organizational initiatives advancing with strategic people planning - [ ] Junior HRBPs developing faster through mentorship and role modeling - [ ] Cross-functional alignment on complex people initiatives achieved - [ ] Recognized as a strategic advisor whose judgment carries weight at the executive level

60-Day Check-in

Manager Discussion Points: - How is executive advisory partnership developing? - What strategic impact have you had on organizational decisions? - Where are you driving the most value and where do you want to expand? - How are you developing other HRBPs? - Feedback on executive influence and strategic leadership

Success Indicators: - [ ] Full ownership of people strategy for supported business segments - [ ] Executive leaders treating Senior HRBP as indispensable strategic advisor - [ ] Complex organizational challenges being addressed with IC4-level judgment - [ ] Mentoring and developing other HRBPs - [ ] Contributing at IC4 level consistently

Days 61-90: Own

Theme: Drive Strategic Impact

Primary Objectives: - Operate fully at IC4 level as trusted executive advisor - Demonstrate people strategies that measurably contribute to business outcomes - Drive lasting structural improvements to how the business manages talent - Establish trajectory for sustained strategic impact

Weeks 9-10: Full IC4 Operation

Activities: - Own people strategy end-to-end for major business segments with full executive partnership - Drive the most consequential organizational decisions—large-scale org design, executive talent strategy, culture transformation - Lead organizational health management at a systemic level—root causes addressed, not just symptoms - Ensure calibration processes produce fair, defensible outcomes that build manager capability - Champion enterprise HR transformations within supported segments with genuine adoption

IC4 Expectations Met: - [ ] VP/SVP leaders treat Senior HRBP as indispensable strategic advisor on all organizational decisions - [ ] People strategies demonstrably contributing to business outcomes with measurable improvements - [ ] Organizational design initiatives executed with minimal disruption and outcomes that hold up over time - [ ] Workforce planning operating at strategic level—anticipating gaps 12-24 months out - [ ] The most complex ER and organizational situations handled with exceptional judgment

Weeks 11-12: Setting Long-Term Trajectory

Activities: - Document strategic people vision for supported business segments over next 12-24 months - Set goals for next quarter focused on enterprise-level impact - Discuss continued development—deeper executive partnership, M&A expertise, organizational transformation leadership - Solidify position as trusted executive advisor - Fully transition from "new hire" mindset

Looking Ahead: - [ ] Clear strategic vision for people strategy across supported segments - [ ] Development plan established for continued growth at IC4 level - [ ] Executive relationships solidified as genuine thought partnerships - [ ] Known as the trusted strategic advisor whose judgment is sought on consequential decisions - [ ] Positioned for continued enterprise-level impact

90-Day Review

Manager Discussion Points: - Comprehensive onboarding assessment - Executive partnership quality and business leader feedback - Organizational health and talent strategy impact - HRBP mentorship and team development contribution - Path forward and strategic priorities

Success Indicators: - [ ] Operating fully at IC4 level - [ ] Strategic ownership clearly established with executive partners - [ ] Organizational influence demonstrated at senior leadership level - [ ] Strong executive relationships and trusted advisor reputation - [ ] Clear on continued strategic impact path

Key Relationships to Build

Who Why When to Meet
Direct manager Strategic alignment, organizational navigation Weekly
VP/SVP business leaders Executive partnership, organizational decisions Weekly or biweekly
CHRO/HR VP Enterprise strategy alignment, sponsorship Biweekly or monthly
Peer Senior HRBPs Cross-segment coordination, pattern sharing Biweekly
Legal counsel Complex ER, compliance, M&A First month, as needed
Finance leadership Workforce planning, compensation strategy, budgeting First month, ongoing

Common IC4 Onboarding Challenges

Challenge Solution
Executives testing or not yet trusting the new Senior HRBP Lead with business insight, not HR jargon; demonstrate value through quality of strategic counsel
Inheriting complex political dynamics between leaders Listen extensively before acting; understand history and power dynamics before intervening
Scope is ambiguous between strategic and operational Clarify expectations with manager; intentionally delegate operational work to free strategic capacity
Mentoring other HRBPs while still onboarding yourself Start with knowledge sharing from fresh perspective; deepen coaching as your context grows
Pressure to demonstrate impact immediately at executive level Balance quick wins with longer-term strategic bets; some interventions need runway to show results
M&A or restructuring dropped on you early Leverage prior experience; be transparent about what context you still need; ask for appropriate support

Manager Checklist

Pre-Arrival

  • [ ] Supported business segments identified with executive alignment
  • [ ] Thorough handoff from previous Senior HRBP or interim support
  • [ ] Executive meetings scheduled with VP/SVP leaders
  • [ ] Sensitive situations, active ER cases, and organizational context documented
  • [ ] Strategic priorities and expectations aligned with HR leadership

Week 1

  • [ ] Executive introductions complete
  • [ ] Business strategy and organizational context transferred
  • [ ] Talent and organizational health data shared
  • [ ] Active situations and pending decisions briefed
  • [ ] Regular cadence established with manager and key executives

Ongoing

  • [ ] Strategic ownership transferring at appropriate pace
  • [ ] Executive influence opportunities provided
  • [ ] Complex organizational challenges supported with appropriate latitude
  • [ ] HRBP mentoring relationships forming
  • [ ] Course corrections made as needed
