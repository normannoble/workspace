Senior HR Manager — Human Resources

The Opportunity

You'll lead a team of 8-15 HR professionals — potentially including other managers — with the independence and cross-discipline fluency to drive real outcomes across the HR function. This isn't a role where someone tells you what to do and checks your work. You'll own significant HR programs end-to-end, build high-performing teams that deliver through demanding business cycles, and develop the next generation of HR managers. If you want to lead with autonomy, shape how HR operates at scale, and be trusted to make the calls that matter, this is it.

What You'll Do

  • Build teams that business leaders fight to partner with — You'll create HR teams known for excellence — high engagement, strong delivery, low regrettable attrition, and the kind of culture where people genuinely grow. Business leaders will trust and seek your teams out.
  • Handle the hard people stuff independently — Performance issues, retention risks, difficult conversations, promotions, terminations — you'll manage the full spectrum of people decisions with confidence and sound judgment, without needing your manager to validate every call.
  • Own programs that move the needle — You'll take end-to-end ownership of significant HR programs or functional areas, ensuring quality, measurable outcomes, and effective coordination across Talent Acquisition, HR Operations, HRBP, L&D, and Compensation & Benefits.
  • Develop managers into leaders — You'll coach M1 HR Managers to grow beyond task execution into genuine people leadership — building their judgment, delegation skills, and confidence through stretch assignments and real-time feedback.
  • Partner at the leadership table — You'll represent the HR function credibly in cross-functional forums, build strong partnerships with business leaders and functions like Finance and Legal, and ensure HR strategy aligns with where the organization is heading.

What You Bring

  • 8-12 years of HR experience across multiple disciplines, with 3-5 years in HR management showing demonstrated growth in scope and capability
  • Track record of building, inheriting and improving, or scaling HR teams across disciplines
  • Experience handling complex people situations: performance management, conflict resolution, retention challenges, and managing through organizational change
  • Demonstrated ability to hire and develop HR professionals who grew in their careers, including developing emerging managers
  • Broad HR knowledge with deep expertise in at least one discipline and working fluency across Talent Acquisition, HR Operations, HRBP, L&D, and Compensation & Benefits
  • Data fluency — comfortable using HR analytics and operational metrics to inform decisions and build business cases
  • Strong stakeholder management skills at the senior leadership level

Nice to Have

  • Experience with organizational design, change management, or strategic HR work beyond operations
  • Senior HR certifications (SHRM-SCP, SPHR)
  • Track record managing through full HR business cycles with teams that delivered consistently

Why [Company]

  • Real autonomy, real impact — You'll have the space to lead your way, make meaningful decisions, and drive outcomes without micromanagement. We trust our senior HR managers to own their domains.
  • A function where management is valued — We invest in developing managers at every level. You'll have peers to learn from, executive sponsors who care about your growth, and the resources to develop your own managers effectively.
  • HR as strategic partner — Our HR function has a seat at the table. The programs you build and the teams you lead will directly shape business outcomes, not just support them from the sidelines.

How to Apply

Submit your application through our careers page. Our process includes a conversation with the hiring manager, a leadership case discussion focused on real HR management scenarios, and meetings with cross-functional partners. We aim to move efficiently and keep you informed throughout — expect a decision within two weeks of your final interview.
