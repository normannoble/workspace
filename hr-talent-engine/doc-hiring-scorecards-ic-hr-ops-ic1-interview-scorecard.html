Level Context: What IC1 Looks Like

IC1 is an early-career HR operations contributor focused on learning the fundamentals of HR operations while contributing reliably to well-defined tasks with appropriate guidance. Evaluate for:

  • Data accuracy — Maintains employee records and HR data with careful attention to detail
  • Process adherence — Follows established SOPs and checklists consistently
  • Learning orientation — Asks thoughtful questions, incorporates feedback, and demonstrates growing competence
  • Professional discretion — Handles sensitive employee information with appropriate care

Differentiation from non-hire: IC1 demonstrates genuine interest in HR operations, attention to detail, and the ability to learn and improve with guidance. Differentiation from IC2: IC1 completes well-defined tasks with guidance; IC2 manages HR processes end-to-end independently.

Competency Assessment

1. HR Systems & Data Accuracy

What to assess: Attention to detail in data entry, ability to learn HRIS platforms, and care for data integrity.

IC1 Expectations: - Employee records and HR data maintained with a high degree of accuracy through careful data entry and attention to detail - Basic HRIS transactions (new hires, terminations, status changes, personal data updates) processed correctly and within expected timeframes - A growing familiarity with the HRIS platform, its core modules, and how data flows between systems - Data discrepancies flagged promptly when identified during routine tasks, with appropriate escalation

Sample Questions: - Describe a time you were responsible for maintaining accurate data or records. How did you ensure quality? - Tell me about a data error you caught before it caused a downstream problem. What alerted you to the issue? - How do you approach learning a new software system or database? Walk me through your process. - What steps do you take to verify your work when entering information into a system?

2. Process Execution & Task Reliability

What to assess: Ability to follow established procedures consistently, manage routine tasks, and communicate proactively about progress and blockers.

IC1 Expectations: - Clearly defined operational tasks completed consistently, following established standard operating procedures and checklists - Routine employee lifecycle transactions (onboarding paperwork, benefits enrollment support, offboarding checklists) executed reliably with minimal rework - Proactive communication about progress, blockers, and when help is needed to keep workflows moving - A growing understanding of end-to-end HR processes and how individual tasks contribute to broader operational outcomes

Sample Questions: - Tell me about a time you followed a detailed checklist or procedure to complete a multi-step task. How did you ensure nothing was missed? - Describe a situation where you had to manage several routine tasks simultaneously. How did you stay organized? - How do you communicate with your manager or team when you are stuck or behind on a task? - Give me an example of a time you noticed that a routine task you were doing connected to a larger process or goal. 3. Compliance & Confidentiality

What to assess: Awareness of employment documentation requirements, care for confidential information, and developing understanding of compliance obligations.

IC1 Expectations: - Required employment documents collected, verified, and filed in accordance with established procedures and regulatory requirements - Sensitive employee information handled with appropriate care, following data privacy and confidentiality protocols - Process documentation and checklists maintained accurately, flagging gaps or outdated steps as encountered - A developing awareness of key compliance obligations (I-9, benefits administration timelines, record retention) and why they matter

Sample Questions: - How do you handle sensitive or confidential information in a work setting? Give me an example. - Describe a time you had to follow a specific regulatory or compliance requirement in your work. What did you do to ensure you met the standard? - What would you do if you noticed a document was missing from an employee file that was supposed to be there? - Why do you think data accuracy and record-keeping are important in HR operations?

4. Learning Agility & Growth Mindset

What to assess: Receptiveness to feedback, curiosity about HR operations, and ability to develop skills over time.

IC1 Expectations: - Feedback and coaching on accuracy and process adherence incorporated effectively - Curiosity and genuine interest in HR operations and people processes - Self-awareness about knowledge gaps and appropriate judgment about when to ask for help - Persistence in working through challenges with appropriate support

Sample Questions: - Tell me about a time you received constructive feedback on your work. How did you respond? - Describe a situation where you had to learn a new domain or process quickly. What was your approach? - How do you decide when to ask for help versus try to figure something out on your own? - What interests you about HR operations as a career path?

5. Stakeholder Service & Collaboration

What to assess: Ability to respond to routine HR inquiries, collaborate with team members, and build effective working relationships.

IC1 Expectations: - Routine HR inquiries from employees and managers resolved accurately and in a timely manner through established service channels - Positive contributions to the HR operations team through curiosity, reliability, and consistent effort - Effective working relationships with immediate team members built on clear communication and follow-through - Inquiries beyond current knowledge or authority escalated appropriately, with relevant context gathered to support efficient resolution

Sample Questions: - Describe a time you helped a colleague or customer with an inquiry or request. How did you ensure they got what they needed? - Tell me about a time you did not have the answer to someone's question. What did you do? - How do you build trust with new colleagues or team members? - Give me an example of a time you contributed positively to a team's culture or morale.
