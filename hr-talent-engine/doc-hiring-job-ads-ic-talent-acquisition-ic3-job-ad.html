Senior Recruiter — Human Resources

The Opportunity

You'll own end-to-end hiring for the roles that matter most — critical, complex, and hard-to-fill positions where your sourcing expertise, hiring manager partnerships, and candidate judgment make the difference between a great hire and a missed opportunity. This isn't just about filling requisitions; you'll shape how the organization attracts and evaluates talent, mentor growing recruiters, and serve as a strategic advisor to business leaders on their toughest talent challenges. If you want deep recruiting work with real influence and the autonomy to run your practice, this is it.

What You'll Do

  • Land the hires that transform teams — You'll fill critical roles with candidates who pass probation with distinction, earn strong early performance reviews, and stay. Your sourcing strategies reach passive talent in competitive markets, and your evaluation rigor ensures quality that holds up over time.
  • Build pipelines before the requisition opens — You'll develop robust, diverse candidate pipelines for recurring and hard-to-fill roles through direct outreach, referral programs, events, and talent mapping. When a new req opens, you'll already have a head start.
  • Be the strategic partner hiring managers actually want — You'll move beyond order-taking to genuine advisory: co-designing roles, calibrating expectations, training interview panels, and driving accountability for structured evaluation. Hiring managers will seek your input, not just your submittals.
  • Elevate the entire recruiting function — You'll mentor junior and mid-level recruiters, share talent market intelligence with leadership, and drive process improvements that raise hiring quality across the team — not just on your own requisitions.
  • Create a candidate experience that builds your employer brand — Even candidates who don't get the offer will leave as advocates for the organization. Your process is transparent, respectful, and moves at a pace that wins talent.

What You Bring

  • 4-7 years of professional recruiting experience, including at least 2 years in a senior or lead capacity
  • Deep proficiency in full-cycle recruiting with strong command of sourcing techniques — boolean search, direct outreach, talent mapping, and passive candidate engagement
  • Experience with structured interviewing methodology, competency-based evaluation, and bias mitigation
  • A track record of filling complex, senior, or specialized roles in competitive talent markets
  • Stakeholder management skills — you build trust with hiring managers, influence role design, and push back constructively when needed
  • Experience with modern ATS platforms and sourcing tools (LinkedIn Recruiter, SeekOut, or equivalent)
  • An ownership mentality — you take accountability for hiring outcomes, not just activity metrics

Nice to Have

  • Experience building and maintaining diverse candidate pipelines through proactive sourcing strategies
  • Familiarity with recruiting analytics, pipeline metrics, and data-driven decision-making
  • History of mentoring junior recruiters and contributing to team capability development

Why [Company]

  • The hardest hiring problems, not the routine ones — You'll work on the roles that require real expertise: competitive-market talent, specialized skills, and positions that shape organizational capability. No assembly-line recruiting.
  • A team that makes each other better — You'll work alongside recruiters who share intelligence, calibrate together, and invest in each other's growth. Your mentorship of others is valued, not just your personal output.
  • Your influence extends beyond your requisitions — We want recruiters who elevate the function, not just fill seats. Your process improvements, market insights, and evaluation standards will shape how the whole team operates.

How to Apply

Submit your application through our careers page. Our process includes a conversation with the hiring manager, a sourcing and strategy deep-dive, and a team fit conversation. We move at a pace that respects your time — expect a decision within two weeks of your final interview.
