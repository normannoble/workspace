HR Operations Specialist

Level: IC2 (Professional)

Role Overview

The HR Operations Specialist is a solid operational contributor who independently manages HR processes end-to-end across assigned areas. This role focuses on building deep proficiency in HRIS administration, compliance reporting, and employee lifecycle management while developing the judgment and consistency needed for growing independence and impact.

Success in this role requires reliable delivery of accurate work, a thorough understanding of how individual processes connect to broader HR operations, and proactive communication about risks and improvement opportunities. HR Operations Specialists at this level are expected to manage routine and moderately complex transactions independently while knowing when to seek guidance on unfamiliar or high-stakes situations.

Success Outcomes

Success in this role means achieving the following outcomes:

HR Systems & Data Integrity

  • HRIS administered effectively for assigned areas, with transactions processed accurately, completely, and within established SLAs
  • Data quality maintained through regular audits, reconciliation activities, and timely correction of identified discrepancies
  • Reports and data extracts produced reliably for stakeholders, with a growing understanding of how HR data supports business decisions
  • System configurations and workflows maintained within assigned areas, with changes tested and documented appropriately

Process Design & Operational Efficiency

  • Employee lifecycle processes (onboarding, transfers, promotions, offboarding) managed end-to-end with consistent quality and minimal rework
  • Opportunities to streamline repetitive tasks or eliminate manual steps identified and raised, with proposed solutions where possible
  • Standard operating procedures maintained and improved based on practical experience administering the processes
  • Self-directed task management and reliable execution across multiple concurrent workflows and deadlines

Compliance & Risk Management

  • Compliance reporting (EEO, benefits filings, audit requests) completed accurately and on schedule for assigned areas
  • Employment records and documentation maintained in accordance with federal, state, and local regulatory requirements
  • Potential compliance gaps or risks identified during routine work and escalated with relevant context before they become issues
  • A solid working knowledge of key employment regulations and how they translate into operational requirements

Stakeholder Service & Collaboration

  • Escalated HR inquiries from employees and managers resolved thoroughly, with clear communication and appropriate follow-up
  • Effective partnerships with HR Centers of Excellence (benefits, talent acquisition, compensation) to coordinate cross-functional processes
  • Process handoffs and dependencies managed proactively, ensuring smooth end-to-end experiences for employees and managers
  • Beginning to support more junior team members with process guidance and knowledge sharing when opportunities arise

Required Skills

HR Operations Expertise

  • Proficiency in HRIS administration with solid understanding of employee data management and system workflows
  • Working knowledge of employee lifecycle processes from hire to separation
  • Familiarity with compliance reporting requirements and employment record-keeping standards
  • Ability to generate, interpret, and validate HR reports and data sets
  • Understanding of benefits administration fundamentals and payroll data integration points

Leadership & Collaboration

  • Clear communication of operational status, risks, and process issues to team members and stakeholders
  • Effective collaboration with HR partners and adjacent functions to deliver seamless employee experiences
  • Good judgment about priorities and trade-offs when managing competing deadlines
  • Reliable self-management, accurate estimation of effort, and consistent follow-through on commitments

Core Competencies

  • Problem-solving skills with ability to diagnose process breakdowns and identify root causes in moderately complex situations
  • Receptiveness to feedback and demonstrated growth in accuracy, efficiency, and process knowledge
  • Initiative in learning new system capabilities, regulations, and operational best practices

Required Experience

Years of Experience

  • 2-4 years of professional HR operations or HRIS administration experience

Demonstrated Track Record

  • Consistent delivery of accurate, timely work in a professional HR operations environment
  • Growing independence over time, with reduced need for oversight on established processes and familiar transaction types
  • Positive feedback from stakeholders and collaborative working relationships across HR functions

Domain Experience

  • Hands-on experience administering an HRIS platform (Workday, ADP, UKG, BambooHR, or similar)
  • Experience managing employee lifecycle transactions in a professional setting
  • Exposure to compliance reporting, audit support, or regulatory record-keeping requirements

Education

  • Bachelor's degree in Human Resources, Business Administration, or related field—or equivalent practical experience
