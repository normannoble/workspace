L&D Coordinator

Level: IC1 (Associate)

Role Overview

The L&D Coordinator is an early-career learning professional focused on building foundational skills in training operations, program logistics, and learning administration. This role ensures that training sessions run smoothly, learner records are accurate, and the operational backbone of the L&D function is reliable and well-organized.

Success in this role is defined by growing competence, not expertise. L&D Coordinators are expected to ask thoughtful questions, develop an understanding of how learning programs are designed and delivered, and demonstrate increasing capability over time. With structured guidance from more senior L&D team members, they build the knowledge and judgment needed to take on program design and delivery responsibilities in the future.

Success Outcomes

Success in this role means achieving the following outcomes:

Training Logistics & Coordination

  • Training sessions that are well-organized with all logistics handled—room bookings, materials prepared, technology tested, and facilitators briefed—so programs run without operational friction
  • Scheduling that accounts for participant availability, facilitator calendars, and business cycles—resulting in strong attendance and minimal conflicts
  • Vendor and facilitator coordination that is timely and professional—contracts tracked, materials received, and communications clear
  • A reliable process for managing training logistics that requires less oversight over time as patterns and best practices are internalized

LMS Administration & Compliance Tracking

  • Learner records in the LMS that are accurate, up to date, and audit-ready—enrollments processed, completions recorded, and data discrepancies resolved promptly
  • Compliance training tracked with clear visibility into completion rates, upcoming deadlines, and at-risk employees—enabling the team to act before deadlines are missed
  • Course catalog and content library maintained in an organized state—new programs added correctly, retired content archived, and metadata kept current
  • LMS support provided to learners and managers that resolves common issues quickly and escalates technical problems appropriately

Program Support & Materials Management

  • Training materials—handouts, slide decks, workbooks, and digital resources—prepared accurately and delivered on time for each program
  • Participant communications—invitations, reminders, pre-work, and follow-ups—sent consistently and clearly, contributing to a professional learner experience
  • Post-training evaluations collected and compiled into summary reports that the team can use to assess program effectiveness
  • A growing library of templates, checklists, and process documentation that makes recurring coordination tasks more efficient

Learning & Professional Development

  • A developing understanding of adult learning principles and instructional design concepts that informs how coordination work is approached
  • Growing familiarity with L&D tools, technologies, and methodologies through observation, participation, and self-directed learning
  • Feedback from senior team members incorporated effectively, with visible improvement in quality and efficiency of coordination work over time
  • Initiative demonstrated in identifying process improvements and volunteering for stretch assignments that build new capabilities

Required Skills

L&D Foundation

  • Basic understanding of training program logistics—scheduling, materials preparation, and session coordination
  • Familiarity with learning management systems or similar administrative platforms, with ability to learn new tools quickly
  • Awareness of compliance training concepts and why accurate record-keeping matters
  • Attention to detail in data entry, scheduling, and materials preparation

Professional Skills

  • Clear written and verbal communication—can send professional participant communications and coordinate with facilitators and vendors
  • Organizational skills and ability to manage multiple concurrent training events and deadlines
  • Receptiveness to feedback and coaching from more experienced L&D professionals
  • Basic problem-solving within defined parameters—can troubleshoot common logistics and LMS issues

Core Competencies

  • Curiosity and genuine interest in how people learn and develop
  • Reliability and follow-through on commitments—the team can count on coordination tasks being completed accurately and on time
  • Adaptability when plans change—training schedules shift, technology fails, or participant needs evolve
  • Attention to detail and care for the learner experience, even in administrative tasks

Required Experience

Years of Experience

  • 0-2 years of professional experience in L&D, HR, training coordination, event planning, or a related administrative field

Background

  • Recent graduate, career changer, or early-career professional with interest in learning and development
  • Experience in administrative coordination, event planning, or project support in any professional context
  • Demonstrated organizational skills through academic work, internships, or prior roles

Technical Exposure

  • Familiarity with office productivity tools (spreadsheets, presentation software, email) for managing training logistics
  • Exposure to any learning management system, content platform, or similar administrative tool (professional or academic context)
  • Basic comfort with data entry, reporting, and maintaining accurate records

Education

  • Bachelor's degree in Human Resources, Education, Business, Communications, or related field—or equivalent practical experience
