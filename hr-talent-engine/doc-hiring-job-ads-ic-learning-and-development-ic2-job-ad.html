L&D Specialist — Human Resources

The Opportunity

You'll design and deliver training programs that help employees build real skills — creating e-learning content, facilitating workshops, and owning program areas where your instructional design choices directly affect how well people learn and grow. This isn't a support role; you'll have ownership over defined program areas, the creative freedom to design engaging learning experiences, and the space to grow toward the strategic needs analysis and program design that define senior L&D work. If you have solid instructional design skills, growing facilitation confidence, and the drive to create learning that actually changes behavior — not just checks boxes — this is your role.

What You'll Do

  • Design training that people actually learn from — You'll create programs with clear learning objectives, well-designed activities, and logical content flow grounded in adult learning principles. Your work will include both instructor-led workshops and e-learning content built with modern authoring tools.
  • Facilitate workshops with growing confidence — You'll lead training sessions for varied audiences, managing group dynamics, keeping content on track, and adapting delivery when participants need more or less depth. Each session makes you a stronger facilitator.
  • Measure what matters and improve continuously — You'll collect post-training evaluations, analyze completion data, and identify what's working and what needs adjustment. Your programs will get better over time because you use evidence, not assumptions.
  • Keep content relevant and stakeholders informed — You'll maintain program materials based on feedback and changing business needs, communicate program status clearly to stakeholders, and gather input from managers and subject matter experts that makes content more practical and credible.

What You Bring

  • 2-4 years of professional experience in L&D, instructional design, training delivery, or a closely related field
  • Proficiency in instructional design principles — you can structure learning objectives, design activities, and sequence content for adult learners
  • Working knowledge of e-learning authoring tools (Articulate, Captivate, or equivalent)
  • Facilitation skills sufficient to lead standard training sessions and workshops
  • Clear communication skills for coordinating with stakeholders, subject matter experts, and cross-functional partners
  • Self-management skills — you estimate effort accurately, manage program timelines, and follow through on commitments
  • Curiosity and initiative in learning new L&D methodologies and tools

Nice to Have

  • Experience with LMS administration, course setup, and basic reporting
  • Track record of improving programs based on learner feedback and evaluation data
  • Background working with subject matter experts to develop technically accurate content

Why [Company]

  • Own programs, not just tasks — You'll have defined program areas where your design decisions shape the learning experience. This is creative, impactful work with real ownership.
  • Learn by doing — Every workshop you facilitate and every program you iterate on builds your skills. We provide feedback, observation, and coaching to accelerate your development as a designer and facilitator.
  • Your work connects to business outcomes — We care about learning that changes behavior, not just courses that get completed. You'll see how your programs affect the teams and individuals who go through them.

How to Apply

Submit your application through our careers page. Our process includes a conversation with the L&D hiring manager, a portfolio review or practical design exercise, and a team fit conversation. We aim to move quickly — expect a decision within two weeks of your final interview.
