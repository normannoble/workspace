Recruiter — Human Resources

The Opportunity

You'll own full-cycle recruiting for roles across the organization — from sourcing and screening through offer close. This isn't a coordination role or an order-taking position; you'll manage your own requisition portfolio, build real partnerships with hiring managers, and develop the sourcing instincts and candidate judgment that define a strong recruiting career. If you want autonomy over your pipeline, genuine ownership of hiring outcomes, and room to grow into a strategic recruiting partner, this is where you build that foundation.

What You'll Do

  • Fill roles with people who actually succeed — You'll manage your requisition portfolio end-to-end, delivering qualified candidates who meet role requirements and are well-calibrated to hiring manager expectations. Your hires stick because you screen for real fit, not just keywords.
  • Build pipelines that keep hiring on track — You'll source candidates through direct outreach, LinkedIn, job boards, and referrals — developing a feel for which channels work for which roles. Your pipelines will be active, diverse, and responsive to market conditions.
  • Become a hiring manager's trusted partner — You'll run effective intake meetings, set realistic timelines, communicate pipeline health clearly, and coordinate smooth interview processes. Over time, you'll move from executing requirements to advising on candidate quality and market dynamics.
  • Deliver a candidate experience worth talking about — You'll keep candidates informed, engaged, and respected at every stage — including timely, thoughtful rejections. Your process moves at a pace that wins talent, not loses it.

What You Bring

  • 2-4 years of professional recruiting experience, including full-cycle responsibility for multiple concurrent requisitions
  • Proficiency with sourcing techniques — boolean search, LinkedIn outreach, and job board optimization
  • Experience with at least one modern ATS platform (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday Recruiting, iCIMS, or similar)
  • Strong communication skills — you can update a hiring manager, pitch a role to a passive candidate, and deliver a rejection with equal clarity
  • The ability to manage a requisition portfolio independently, prioritize effectively, and follow through on commitments
  • Good judgment about candidate quality — you assess beyond surface qualifications

Nice to Have

  • Experience recruiting across multiple functions or role types
  • Familiarity with structured interviewing practices and bias mitigation
  • Track record of improving sourcing response rates or pipeline conversion metrics

Why [Company]

  • Real ownership, not ticket-taking — You'll manage your own portfolio with genuine autonomy. We trust recruiters to make decisions, not just follow scripts.
  • Hiring managers who want to partner — Our managers view recruiting as a collaboration, not a service desk. You'll have engaged stakeholders who value your input.
  • Growth into strategic recruiting — We invest in developing recruiters from pipeline executors into talent advisors. Your trajectory here leads to increasingly complex, high-impact work.

How to Apply

Submit your application through our careers page. Our process includes a conversation with the hiring manager, a practical recruiting scenario exercise, and a team fit conversation. We aim to move quickly and respect your time — expect a decision within two weeks of your final interview.
