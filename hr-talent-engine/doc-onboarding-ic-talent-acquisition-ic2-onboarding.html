30-60-90 Day Onboarding: Recruiter (IC2)

Overview

Level: IC2 — Recruiter Focus: Building full-cycle recruiting capability, developing hiring manager partnerships, establishing independent execution Manager's Role: Supportive guide, sounding board, increasing autonomy with coaching

IC2 Recruiters are solid professionals who independently manage full-cycle recruiting for standard roles. Onboarding provides structured exposure to the organization's hiring landscape, tools, and stakeholders, then quickly transitions to independent requisition management. The goal is to move from learning the environment to delivering quality hires independently within 90 days.

Days 1-30: Learn

Theme: Learn the Basics

Primary Objectives: - Understand the organization's hiring needs, culture, and talent strategy - Learn the ATS, sourcing tools, and recruiting processes specific to this organization - Build relationships with hiring managers and recruiting team members - Observe and understand current pipeline health and open requisitions

Week 1: Strategic Orientation

Day Focus Activities
1 Welcome Team introductions, org overview, company culture and values deep-dive, tool access setup
2 Recruiting Process End-to-end recruiting workflow, ATS configuration, sourcing tool stack, reporting dashboards
3 Hiring Landscape Review open requisitions, understand hiring priorities, key role types, and hiring volume
4 Stakeholders Meet hiring managers for assigned departments, understand their teams and hiring history
5 Sourcing & Brand Employer brand messaging, job posting standards, sourcing channels, outreach templates

Key Meetings to Schedule: - [ ] Manager 1:1 (weekly) - [ ] 1:1 with each recruiting team member - [ ] Key hiring managers for assigned requisitions - [ ] TA leadership for function strategy overview - [ ] HR Business Partners for department context - [ ] Compensation team for offer process and ranges

Weeks 2-4: Deep Immersion

Learning Goals: - [ ] Proficient in the ATS—managing requisitions, tracking candidates, generating pipeline reports - [ ] Familiar with sourcing tools (LinkedIn Recruiter, job boards) and outreach best practices - [ ] Understand structured interviewing approach and evaluation criteria used here - [ ] Know the offer approval process, compensation philosophy, and competitive positioning - [ ] Clear on hiring manager expectations and how the TA team measures success

Activities: - Shadow senior recruiters through intake calls, screens, and debriefs - Begin sourcing for 1-2 requisitions to build pipeline familiarity - Conduct initial phone screens under guidance with debrief afterward - Review recent closed requisitions to understand what "good" looks like - Document key processes, hiring manager preferences, and role-specific notes

Milestones: - [ ] First phone screens conducted independently with calibrated feedback - [ ] Sourcing producing qualified candidates for assigned requisitions - [ ] ATS pipeline management accurate and current - [ ] Can articulate employer value proposition for assigned role types - [ ] Comfortable with intake meeting format and expectations

30-Day Check-in

Manager Discussion Points: - How comfortable are you with the tools and recruiting process? - What is your initial assessment of pipeline health for assigned requisitions? - How are hiring manager relationships developing? - Where do you need more context or training? - Initial feedback on screening quality and sourcing effectiveness

Success Indicators: - [ ] ATS and sourcing tools used confidently and accurately - [ ] Initial phone screens calibrated to team standards - [ ] Hiring manager relationships started with clear communication - [ ] Understands the organization's recruiting approach and standards - [ ] Proactively managing assigned pipeline with appropriate urgency

Days 31-60: Contribute

Theme: Start Delivering

Primary Objectives: - Manage full-cycle recruiting independently for assigned requisitions - Build consistent sourcing pipelines and maintain candidate engagement - Develop hiring manager partnerships beyond order-taking toward advisory - Deliver quality candidate submittals and move processes forward at pace

Weeks 5-6: Independent Full-Cycle Execution

Activities: - Own full recruiting cycle for assigned requisitions—sourcing through offer - Conduct intake meetings independently, aligning on requirements and timelines - Source proactively using multiple channels—LinkedIn, job boards, referrals, outreach - Manage candidate experience with responsive, organized communication - Present candidates to hiring managers with clear, calibrated assessments

Demonstrating Full-Cycle Capability: - [ ] Requisitions managed end-to-end with quality candidates submitted on timeline - [ ] Sourcing techniques applied independently with growing effectiveness - [ ] Hiring managers receiving consistent updates on pipeline status and market conditions - [ ] Phone screens and candidate assessments calibrated and well-documented - [ ] Candidate communications professional, timely, and reflecting employer brand

Weeks 7-8: Building Consistency

Activities: - Expand requisition load to full portfolio as confidence grows - Begin advising hiring managers on candidate quality and market conditions - Optimize sourcing strategies based on pipeline data and response rates - Coordinate interview processes smoothly—panels prepared, feedback collected promptly - Track personal recruiting metrics and identify areas for improvement

Growing Impact: - [ ] Offer acceptance rates meeting or approaching team benchmarks - [ ] Hiring managers requesting updates less often because communication is proactive - [ ] Pipeline diversity improving through intentional sourcing strategies - [ ] Interview processes running smoothly with minimal coordination gaps - [ ] Growing awareness of talent market conditions for assigned role types

60-Day Check-in

Manager Discussion Points: - How is your requisition portfolio feeling—manageable, challenging, overwhelming? - What is your pipeline health across active requisitions? - How are hiring manager partnerships evolving? - Where are you seeing sourcing challenges or market constraints? - Feedback on hiring quality, process management, and stakeholder communication

Success Indicators: - [ ] Managing full requisition portfolio independently with reliable delivery - [ ] Sourcing pipelines active for each open requisition - [ ] Hiring managers view you as a reliable, communicative recruiting partner - [ ] Candidate experience is responsive and organized - [ ] Contributing at IC2 level consistently across the recruiting cycle

Days 61-90: Own

Theme: Become Independent

Primary Objectives: - Operate fully at IC2 level with consistent delivery and quality - Demonstrate growing sophistication in candidate assessment and market awareness - Establish yourself as a reliable recruiting partner hiring managers trust - Set trajectory for continued development toward senior recruiting capability

Weeks 9-10: Full IC2 Operation

Activities: - Manage full portfolio with consistent quality, timeliness, and stakeholder satisfaction - Close offers effectively with competitive positioning and strong candidate engagement - Proactively adjust sourcing strategies when initial approaches underperform - Contribute to team discussions on process improvements and best practices - Support coordinators with guidance on scheduling and candidate communication

IC2 Expectations Met: - [ ] Requisitions filled with qualified candidates calibrated to hiring manager expectations - [ ] Portfolio delivered within target timelines with early communication on challenges - [ ] Offer acceptance rates reflecting strong candidate engagement - [ ] Screening and evaluation applied consistently with growing sophistication - [ ] Candidate experience responsive, organized, and on-brand

Weeks 11-12: Setting Long-Term Trajectory

Activities: - Document sourcing playbooks for recurring role types - Set development goals for next quarter - Discuss IC3 Senior Recruiter path if interested—what skills and experiences to pursue - Solidify hiring manager relationships and reputation - Fully transition from "new hire" mindset to established team contributor

Looking Ahead: - [ ] Clear understanding of your recruiting strengths and development areas - [ ] Development plan established with manager - [ ] Hiring manager relationships solidified - [ ] Known as a reliable, consistent recruiter for your assigned areas - [ ] Positioned for continued growth toward IC3

90-Day Review

Manager Discussion Points: - Comprehensive onboarding assessment - Recruiting effectiveness and hiring quality evaluation - Stakeholder partnership and communication feedback - Path forward—deepening IC2 expertise vs. building toward IC3 breadth - Recognition and development areas

Success Indicators: - [ ] Operating fully at IC2 level with consistent, independent delivery - [ ] Full-cycle ownership clearly established for assigned requisitions - [ ] Hiring manager partnerships built on trust and reliable communication - [ ] Strong candidate experience and employer brand representation - [ ] Clear on continued growth path within talent acquisition

Key Relationships to Build

Who Why When to Meet
Direct manager Alignment, feedback, development Weekly
Hiring managers Partnership, intake, calibration Weekly or as requisitions require
Senior recruiters Mentorship, calibration, sourcing tips First month, ongoing
TA coordinators Scheduling partnership, process coordination Ongoing
HR Business Partners Department context, workforce planning awareness First month, quarterly
Compensation team Offer strategy, market data alignment First month, as needed

Common IC2 Onboarding Challenges

Challenge Solution
Unfamiliar with this organization's specific role types Request deep-dives from hiring managers; review past successful hires; study job architectures
Sourcing techniques not calibrated to new talent market Test multiple channels; review team benchmarks; get feedback on outreach messaging
Hiring manager expectations unclear Conduct thorough intake meetings; ask clarifying questions; calibrate after first submittals
Balancing speed with quality under requisition pressure Prioritize with manager; communicate timeline expectations; focus on pipeline quality over volume
Transitioning from previous company's recruiting culture Observe before prescribing; ask about the "why" behind processes; adapt your approach

Manager Checklist

Pre-Arrival

  • [ ] Requisition portfolio identified for initial assignment
  • [ ] ATS access and sourcing tool licenses provisioned
  • [ ] Hiring manager introductions scheduled
  • [ ] First-week shadowing schedule prepared
  • [ ] Team expectations for ramp timeline communicated

Week 1

  • [ ] Orientation and tool onboarding complete
  • [ ] Hiring manager introductions done
  • [ ] Recruiting process and standards reviewed
  • [ ] First sourcing activities started
  • [ ] Weekly 1:1 cadence established

Ongoing

  • [ ] Requisition portfolio expanding at appropriate pace
  • [ ] Hiring quality and pipeline metrics reviewed regularly
  • [ ] Feedback from hiring managers collected and shared
  • [ ] Independence growing with coaching on complex situations
  • [ ] Course corrections made as needed on sourcing, screening, or communication
