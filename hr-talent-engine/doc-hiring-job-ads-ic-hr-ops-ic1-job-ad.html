HR Operations Coordinator — Human Resources

The Opportunity

You'll build the foundation of an HR operations career by keeping the systems, data, and processes that support every employee running accurately and on time. This is where you'll learn how HRIS platforms, employee lifecycle transactions, and compliance requirements actually work — not from a textbook, but by doing the work that matters. With mentorship from experienced HR Ops professionals, you'll develop the accuracy, process knowledge, and judgment that open the door to increasingly impactful work. If you're detail-oriented, curious about how HR systems support an organization, and ready to start a career where precision has real consequences, this is your entry point.

What You'll Do

  • Keep employee data accurate and trustworthy — You'll process HRIS transactions (new hires, terminations, status changes, personal data updates) correctly and on time, building the data integrity that the entire HR function depends on.
  • Support employees through every lifecycle moment — You'll execute onboarding paperwork, benefits enrollment support, and offboarding checklists so that employees' transitions are smooth and nothing falls through the cracks.
  • Protect the organization through careful compliance work — You'll collect, verify, and file employment documents following established procedures and regulatory requirements, handling sensitive information with the care it demands.
  • Grow into an HR operations professional — You'll develop a real understanding of end-to-end HR processes — not just what to do, but why it matters. Every transaction you process builds the knowledge base for more complex, independent work ahead.

What You Bring

  • Strong attention to detail and genuine care for data accuracy — HR data errors have real consequences for employees
  • Clear written and verbal communication skills for coordinating with team members and responding to employee inquiries
  • Comfort working with spreadsheets, databases, and information systems (HRIS experience is a plus, not required)
  • Organizational skills to manage multiple tasks and deadlines with appropriate support
  • Respect for confidentiality and discretion when handling sensitive employee information
  • Curiosity about HR operations and a willingness to learn new systems and processes quickly

Nice to Have

  • Previous experience with an HRIS platform (Workday, ADP, BambooHR, UKG, or similar)
  • Internship or work experience in HR, administrative operations, or data-focused roles
  • Familiarity with employment documentation requirements (I-9, benefits enrollment, record retention)

Why [Company]

  • Learn the systems that power HR — You'll gain hands-on experience with HRIS platforms, compliance processes, and employee lifecycle management that form the operational backbone of any HR function.
  • Mentorship that accelerates your growth — Experienced HR Ops professionals will guide your development with structured feedback and increasing responsibility, not just task assignments.
  • Precision matters here, and so does your growth — We care about getting things right, and we invest in developing people who share that standard. Your path from coordinator to specialist is planned and supported.

How to Apply

Submit your application through our careers page. Our process includes an introductory conversation with the HR Ops team, a practical exercise focused on attention to detail and process execution, and a team culture conversation. We keep you informed throughout and aim for a decision within two weeks of your final interview.
