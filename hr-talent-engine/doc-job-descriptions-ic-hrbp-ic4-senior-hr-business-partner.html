Senior HR Business Partner

Level: IC4 (Expert)

Role Overview

The Senior HR Business Partner is a strategic people leader who partners with VP and SVP-level executives on the most complex organizational challenges—large-scale org design, M&A people integration, culture transformation, and enterprise talent strategy. This role operates at the intersection of deep HR expertise and organizational strategy, shaping how the company builds, develops, and retains talent across entire business segments.

Success in this role requires the ability to influence senior leaders on consequential people decisions, anticipate organizational risks before they materialize, and drive initiatives that create lasting structural improvements to how the business manages talent. Senior HRBPs are recognized as trusted advisors whose judgment on organizational matters carries weight at the executive level. They define the standard for business partnership quality, mentor other HRBPs, and ensure that enterprise HR strategy is informed by the realities of the business units they support.

Success Outcomes

Success in this role means achieving the following outcomes:

Strategic Business Partnership

  • VP/SVP-level leaders who treat the Senior HRBP as an indispensable strategic advisor on organizational decisions—actively involving them in business planning, leadership team discussions, and strategic pivots rather than consulting them only on people-specific issues
  • People strategies for major business segments that demonstrably contribute to business outcomes—with clear connections between organizational interventions and measurable improvements in performance, velocity, or capability
  • Organizational design initiatives at scale (restructurings, M&A integration, new business unit creation) that are executed with minimal disruption, clear accountability, and outcomes that hold up over time—not just on paper but in practice
  • Workforce planning that operates at the strategic level—anticipating capability gaps, leadership pipeline risks, and market dynamics 12-24 months out and translating these into concrete investment decisions for talent acquisition, development, and organizational structure

Talent Management & Development

  • A talent strategy across supported business segments that identifies and accelerates the development of the organization's most critical leaders—with succession depth for key roles that is genuinely ready, not aspirational
  • Executive coaching relationships with senior leaders that measurably improve their effectiveness as people leaders—demonstrated through improved team engagement, reduced regrettable attrition, and stronger talent pipelines in their organizations
  • Talent mobility and development programs at the organizational level that break down silos, create cross-functional career paths, and retain high-potential talent who might otherwise leave for growth opportunities elsewhere
  • Calibration processes across large organizations that produce fair, defensible outcomes and build manager capability—going beyond compliance to create genuine shared understanding of performance standards and talent potential

Employee Relations & Organizational Health

  • The most complex and sensitive employee relations situations (executive misconduct, systemic cultural issues, high-profile departures, workplace investigations with legal exposure) handled with the judgment, discretion, and strategic awareness that protects the organization while treating individuals fairly
  • Organizational health across supported business segments monitored and managed at a systemic level—identifying patterns across teams, leaders, and business cycles that reveal root causes rather than symptoms, and designing interventions that address underlying structural or cultural issues
  • Culture transformation initiatives during periods of significant organizational change (M&A integration, rapid scaling, strategic pivots) that deliberately shape the target culture rather than allowing it to emerge haphazardly—with measurable indicators of cultural alignment tracked over time
  • A consistent standard for how HR policies and practices are applied across large, diverse organizations—with the judgment to know when consistency matters and when context-appropriate flexibility serves the organization better

HR Program Execution & Feedback

  • Enterprise HR strategy influenced through deep business insight—ensuring that company-wide programs (compensation philosophy, performance management design, leadership development architecture) reflect the actual needs and realities of the business, not just HR best practices in the abstract
  • Large-scale HR transformations (new performance management systems, compensation restructuring, HRIS migrations) championed and driven within supported business segments with adoption that reflects genuine behavior change, not just process compliance
  • A feedback loop between business reality and HR Centers of Excellence that is systematic and impactful—not just anecdotal escalations but structured insight that shapes program design, investment priorities, and policy evolution
  • Other HRBPs (IC2s and IC3s) who are developing faster and performing better because of the Senior HRBP's mentorship, pattern-sharing, and role modeling of what excellent business partnership looks like

Required Skills

HR & Business Expertise

  • Expert-level knowledge across HR disciplines with particular depth in organizational design, executive talent management, change management, and employment law as it applies to complex organizational situations
  • Sophisticated business acumen—ability to engage with senior executives on business strategy, financial performance, and competitive dynamics as a peer, not just an HR advisor
  • Deep experience with organizational diagnostics at scale—can identify systemic dysfunction patterns across large organizations and design multi-faceted interventions that address root causes
  • M&A people integration expertise—understanding of cultural assessment, integration planning, retention strategy, and the human dynamics of organizational combination

Leadership & Collaboration

  • Executive influence and advisory skills—can challenge, coach, and redirect VP/SVP-level leaders on sensitive people decisions while maintaining trust and partnership
  • Organizational navigation at the most complex level—understanding power dynamics, competing agendas, and institutional history well enough to drive change through influence rather than authority
  • Mentorship and development of other HRBPs—can articulate what great business partnership looks like, diagnose where developing HRBPs are struggling, and accelerate their growth through coaching, exposure, and feedback
  • Cross-functional leadership with HR Centers of Excellence, Legal, Finance, and executive leadership—building coalitions and aligning stakeholders on complex people initiatives

Core Competencies

  • Exceptional judgment under ambiguity and pressure—can make consequential decisions about organizational matters when information is incomplete, stakes are high, and multiple legitimate interests are in tension
  • Systems thinking at the organizational level—understands how decisions about structure, compensation, leadership, and culture interact across a large organization and over extended time horizons
  • Strategic pattern recognition—can synthesize signals from engagement data, attrition trends, leadership behavior, market dynamics, and business performance into a coherent organizational narrative that guides intervention
  • Resilience and composure in high-stakes situations—can manage executive-level conflicts, sensitive investigations, and organizational crises while maintaining objectivity and credibility

Required Experience

Years of Experience

  • 8-12+ years of progressive HR experience, including at least 4 years in a strategic HRBP role supporting senior leadership (VP-level and above)

Demonstrated Track Record

  • Led large-scale organizational initiatives (org design, M&A integration, culture transformation, major restructuring) with outcomes that held up over time
  • Proven ability to influence senior executives on consequential people decisions—with specific examples of changing leadership direction on talent, structure, or culture matters
  • History of managing the most complex employee relations situations with sound judgment, appropriate outcomes, and minimal organizational risk
  • Track record of developing other HR professionals—HRBPs they have mentored who have grown into stronger business partners as a result
  • Experience shaping enterprise HR strategy through business insight and structured advocacy, not just executing programs designed by others

Domain Experience

  • Deep experience supporting multiple business functions at senior levels, with demonstrated ability to quickly develop credibility in new business domains
  • Hands-on leadership of organizational design at significant scale—not just participating in restructurings but leading the people strategy, communication, and execution
  • Experience with HR analytics at the strategic level—using workforce data to build business cases, forecast organizational risks, and measure the impact of people initiatives
  • Exposure to M&A people integration or other large-scale organizational transformation work

Education

  • Bachelor's degree in Human Resources, Business Administration, Organizational Psychology, Industrial/Organizational Psychology, or related field—or equivalent practical experience
  • HR certifications (SHRM-SCP, SPHR) or advanced degree (MBA, MS in I/O Psychology or OD) are valued but not required
