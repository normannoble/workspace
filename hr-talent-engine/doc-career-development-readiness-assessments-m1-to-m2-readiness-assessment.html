M1 to M2 Readiness Assessment

HR Manager to Senior HR Manager

Field Value
Employee Name
Manager
Date
Time at M1

How to Use This Assessment

This assessment evaluates readiness for Senior HR Manager (M2). For each competency:

  • Employee self-assesses with specific examples of M2-level work
  • Manager assesses based on observed behavior and impact
  • Discuss gaps and identify development actions

Rating Scale

Rating Description
1 - Not Yet Still operating at M1 level; requires significant development
2 - Emerging Shows M2 behaviors inconsistently; needs more practice and experience
3 - Ready Consistently demonstrates M2-level performance in this area

Promotion readiness requires "3 - Ready" in most areas, with no areas at "1 - Not Yet."

1. Independent People Management

M2 expectation: Handles complex people situations independently, makes sound people decisions with confidence, and manages the unique challenge of leading HR professionals who are themselves people experts.

1a. Complex Situation Handling

Does the manager handle difficult performance conversations, conflicts, retention risks, and the unique challenges of managing HR professionals without significant manager oversight?
