E2 to E3 Readiness Assessment

Senior Director of HR to Chief People Officer

Field Value
Candidate
Sponsor

Time at E2
Current Scope

Assessment Context

E3 (Chief People Officer) is the senior-most HR executive role—ultimate accountability for people strategy, organizational culture, talent capabilities, and the health of the employee experience. This assessment evaluates readiness for that level of responsibility.

  • Candidate prepares comprehensive evidence packet documenting CPO-level impact
  • Sponsor (typically CEO) presents case to assessment panel
  • Panel includes board member(s) and external perspective
  • Assessment may include external validation (industry peers, references)

Rating Scale

Rating Description
1 - Not Yet Operating at strong E2 level; CPO-level impact not yet demonstrated
2 - Emerging Shows CPO impact in some areas; needs broader demonstration
3 - Ready Consistently demonstrates CPO-level impact and capability

CPO readiness requires "3 - Ready" in all core areas. This is the highest bar in the organization.

1. People Vision & Strategy

CPO expectation: People vision creating sustainable competitive advantage; investments that prove sound over 3-5+ year horizons; strategic alignment between people capabilities and business strategy.

1a. Competitive Advantage Through People

Has the candidate created people capabilities that are genuine sources of competitive advantage—the company wins in its markets in part because of how it attracts, develops, and retains talent?
