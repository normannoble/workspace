30-60-90 Day Onboarding: Head of HR (M3)

Overview

Level: M3 — Head of HR Focus: Assessing the HR function holistically, developing HR managers at all levels, aligning HR strategy with company objectives, building executive partnerships Manager's Role: Executive sponsor, providing strategic context and organizational access, offering counsel on the most consequential decisions

M3 Heads of HR are senior leaders responsible for a significant portion of or the entire HR function. Onboarding focuses on understanding how the function operates as an integrated system across all five disciplines—Talent Acquisition, HR Operations, HRBP, L&D, and Compensation & Benefits—and assessing organizational health, leadership bench strength, and strategic alignment. The goal is to establish credibility with executive leadership while building a clear picture of where the function excels and where it needs investment.

Days 1-30: Learn

Theme: Assess the Landscape

Primary Objectives: - Understand the HR function's current state—team capabilities, program maturity, and organizational health across all disciplines - Assess M1 and M2 manager strength, leadership culture, and bench depth - Build relationships with executive leadership and key cross-functional partners - Map the strategic context—company objectives, growth plans, and how the HR function needs to evolve to support them

Week 1: Executive Orientation

Day Focus Activities
1 Welcome Executive team introductions, company strategy briefing, HR function overview
2 HR Leadership Meet M1 and M2 managers—understand the leadership team, capabilities, and current challenges
3 Function Review Review HR programs, metrics, compliance posture, and operational health across all five disciplines
4 Business Context Meet business unit leaders—understand workforce needs, HR satisfaction, and strategic priorities
5 Executive Alignment Deep-dive with your manager (CHRO/CPO/CEO) on expectations, organizational dynamics, and strategic priorities

Key Meetings to Schedule: - [ ] Manager 1:1 (weekly) - [ ] 1:1 with each M1 and M2 manager - [ ] Skip-level meetings with key HR professionals across disciplines - [ ] Executive team members (CEO, CFO, CTO, and other C-level leaders) - [ ] Finance, Legal, and IT leadership for cross-functional alignment - [ ] Previous Head of HR or interim leader (if applicable)

Weeks 2-4: Deep Assessment

Learning Goals: - [ ] Comprehensive assessment of HR function capabilities, gaps, and maturity across all five disciplines - [ ] Clear view of management bench strength—which M1s and M2s are ready for growth, which need development - [ ] Understanding of cross-discipline integration—where the function operates as a system and where silos persist - [ ] Assessment of HR function scalability—can it grow with the company without proportional headcount or coordination overhead? - [ ] Map of company culture, engagement trends, compliance posture, and organizational health indicators

Activities: - Review HR metrics, engagement data, attrition patterns, and program effectiveness reports - Observe HR leadership team dynamics in meetings and cross-functional interactions - Assess HRIS infrastructure, analytics capabilities, and technology investments - Evaluate compliance posture across employment law, regulatory requirements, and company policies - Document findings and develop initial strategic assessment

Milestones: - [ ] Leadership cadence established with all direct reports - [ ] Can articulate the function's strengths, gaps, and strategic priorities - [ ] Executive relationships initiated with credibility and mutual understanding - [ ] Understanding of workforce planning needs and talent pipeline health - [ ] Initial strategic assessment drafted (for your own reference and manager discussion)

30-Day Check-in

Manager Discussion Points: - What is your assessment of the HR function's overall health and capability? - Where are the most significant risks—people, programs, compliance, or organizational? - How do you evaluate the HR leadership bench—M1s and M2s? - What cross-functional partnerships need investment? - Initial feedback on contributions

Success Indicators: - [ ] Comprehensive understanding of the HR function across all disciplines - [ ] HR leadership team trusts and respects the new leader - [ ] Executive relationships established with mutual understanding - [ ] Identified the highest-priority strategic and operational improvements - [ ] Operating with executive-level judgment and appropriate pace

Days 31-60: Contribute

Theme: Assess and Improve

Primary Objectives: - Begin developing M1 and M2 managers toward greater leadership capability - Address the highest-priority operational and strategic gaps - Shape HR function culture and establish shared standards and expectations - Strengthen cross-functional partnerships and executive positioning of HR

Weeks 5-6: Strengthening HR Leadership

Activities: - Initiate deliberate development plans for M1 and M2 managers - Address the most pressing cross-discipline coordination gap - Make consequential decisions about HR priorities, resource allocation, or program direction - Establish or refine function-wide standards and practices - Begin shaping the narrative around HR strategy for executive audiences

Demonstrating Senior Leadership: - [ ] M1 and M2 managers receiving coaching that develops their leadership judgment, not just execution - [ ] Strategic priorities clarified and communicated across the HR function - [ ] Cross-discipline integration improved in at least one tangible area - [ ] Resource allocation decisions reflect strategic thinking, not just historical precedent - [ ] Executive team sees HR as increasingly strategic and well-led

Weeks 7-8: Building Organizational Capability

Activities: - Lead a strategic initiative that demonstrates function-wide impact - Drive workforce planning or talent strategy discussions with executive leadership - Build stronger Finance, Legal, and IT partnerships on shared organizational priorities - Assess and begin addressing bench strength and succession gaps - Represent the HR function in company-level strategic forums with credibility

Growing Impact: - [ ] HR function operating with improved coordination and strategic alignment - [ ] Management culture across HR reflecting shared standards and expectations - [ ] Executive partnerships deepening—HR positioned as a strategic function - [ ] Forward-looking planning beginning—anticipating needs 6-18 months ahead - [ ] Organizational health indicators being actively monitored and addressed

60-Day Check-in

Manager Discussion Points: - How is the HR leadership team developing? - What strategic changes have you initiated, and what early results are visible? - How is the HR function's positioning with executive leadership evolving? - Where do you need more authority, resources, or organizational support? - Feedback on strategic leadership and organizational impact

Success Indicators: - [ ] M1 and M2 manager development underway with visible trajectory - [ ] At least one strategic improvement demonstrating function-wide impact - [ ] Executive team increasing confidence in HR leadership and strategy - [ ] Cross-functional partnerships productive and growing - [ ] Operating with strategic judgment appropriate to an M3 role

Days 61-90: Own

Theme: Lead with Confidence

Primary Objectives: - Operate fully at M3 level—leading the HR function with strategic clarity and organizational authority - Drive HR strategy aligned with company objectives with measurable progress - Demonstrate sustained impact through the leadership team, not personal heroics - Establish long-term trajectory for the function and your own leadership

Weeks 9-10: Full M3 Operation

Activities: - Own HR function performance across all disciplines with full accountability - Drive strategic planning for the next 6-18 months aligned with company direction - Manage the HR leadership team—develop M1s and M2s, address performance, build bench - Lead cross-functional initiatives that improve organizational outcomes - Advise executive leadership on the most consequential people and organizational matters

M3 Expectations Met: - [ ] Multiple HR teams delivering consistently against strategic objectives across all five disciplines - [ ] Coordinated execution across disciplines—HR operating as an integrated system - [ ] HR managers growing as leaders with deliberate development and coaching - [ ] HR strategy clearly aligned with company objectives and visible to executive leadership - [ ] Organizational health actively monitored—engagement, retention, culture, compliance

Weeks 11-12: Setting Long-Term Trajectory

Activities: - Develop HR function strategy and roadmap for the next 2-4 quarters - Establish succession plans for key leadership roles within the function - Discuss E1 development path if interested - Solidify position and reputation as a senior organizational leader - Fully transition from "new hire" mindset

Looking Ahead: - [ ] Clear strategic vision for the HR function with executive alignment - [ ] Development plan established for your own leadership growth - [ ] Relationships solidified with executive team, business leaders, and cross-functional partners - [ ] Known as a senior leader who builds organizational capability through people - [ ] Positioned for continued strategic impact at the executive level

90-Day Review

Manager Discussion Points: - Comprehensive onboarding assessment - HR function performance and strategic direction evaluation - Leadership development and bench strength assessment - Cross-functional influence and executive positioning observed - Recognition and growth areas

Success Indicators: - [ ] Operating fully at M3 level with strategic confidence and organizational authority - [ ] HR function ownership clearly established across all disciplines - [ ] Leadership development demonstrating your multiplier effect on the function - [ ] Strong executive relationships and HR positioned as a strategic partner - [ ] Clear on continued growth path

Key Relationships to Build

Who Why When to Meet
Direct manager (CHRO/CPO/CEO) Strategic alignment, organizational context, executive counsel Weekly
M1 and M2 managers Development, delegation, function health Weekly
Executive team members Strategic partnership, company-level influence First month, biweekly or monthly
Finance leadership Budget, workforce planning, shared analytics First month, ongoing
Legal counsel Compliance, employment law, risk management First month, ongoing
Business unit leaders Workforce needs, HR satisfaction, strategic alignment First month, monthly

Common M3 Onboarding Challenges

Challenge Solution
Inheriting uneven management quality across the function Assess honestly; invest in development for those with potential; make tough calls where needed
HR function operating in silos rather than as an integrated system Identify the highest-value integration point; build coordination mechanisms; model cross-discipline thinking
Executive team undervaluing HR as a strategic function Demonstrate strategic thinking early; connect HR work to business outcomes; deliver results before demanding a seat at the table
Balancing immediate operational needs with long-term strategic investment Stabilize operations first; create space for strategic work through better delegation and process
Pace of change—wanting to transform faster than the organization can absorb Sequence changes deliberately; build trust through quick wins before driving larger transformation
Navigating the emotional weight of being responsible for the people function Build peer relationships outside HR; maintain boundaries; model sustainable leadership
