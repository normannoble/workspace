Principal Compensation Analyst — Human Resources

The Opportunity

You'll define how this organization uses compensation as a strategic lever — shaping the total rewards philosophy, designing enterprise frameworks, advising the executive team and board, and navigating the most complex compensation challenges we face. As a Principal Compensation Analyst, you'll architect compensation programs that scale across business units and geographies, lead enterprise pay equity strategy, drive M&A compensation due diligence, and elevate the entire compensation function's capability. This is the role for a compensation strategist who wants to operate at the intersection of pay expertise and business strategy, with the organizational influence to make their vision real.

What You'll Do

  • Define enterprise compensation strategy — You'll shape our compensation philosophy and total rewards framework, ensuring they're aligned with business strategy and consistently reinforced in every pay decision across the organization.
  • Design compensation architectures that scale — You'll build salary structures, job families, and career frameworks that work across business units, geographies, and growth stages — systems that last, not patches that need constant rework.
  • Advise the executive team and board — You'll be the trusted voice on compensation strategy for C-suite leaders and the compensation committee, translating complex data into strategic recommendations that shape workforce investment decisions.
  • Lead enterprise pay equity with best-in-class rigor — You'll own the pay equity strategy end-to-end: methodology, analysis, remediation, and continuous monitoring embedded in organizational culture. This is equity as a strategic commitment, not a compliance exercise.
  • Navigate the hardest compensation challenges — M&A due diligence, international expansion, executive compensation design, pay compression at scale — you'll lead the work that requires the deepest expertise and the most sophisticated judgment.
  • Elevate the compensation function — You'll develop frameworks, methodology, and knowledge that raise the team's capability to handle sophisticated work independently. Your legacy is a function that's stronger because of your leadership.

What You Bring

  • 8-12+ years of progressive compensation experience, including significant time in senior or principal roles with enterprise scope
  • Expert-level proficiency in compensation program design: salary structures, variable pay, equity/LTI plans, executive compensation, and international pay practices
  • Deep pay equity methodology expertise — multivariate regression, intersectional analysis, and enterprise-scale remediation strategy
  • Experience advising C-suite executives and board or compensation committee members on compensation strategy with credibility
  • M&A compensation due diligence experience — harmonization planning, total cost modeling, and integration execution
  • Executive compensation knowledge including governance, proxy disclosure, and say-on-pay dynamics
  • The strategic judgment to connect compensation design to business outcomes and the communication skills to make the case at every level

Nice to Have

  • CCP (Certified Compensation Professional) certification; CECP or GRP a plus
  • Experience across multiple industries or organizational growth stages
  • International compensation experience across multiple jurisdictions

Why [Company]

  • Enterprise strategy, not program administration — You'll define compensation philosophy and design frameworks that shape how the entire organization thinks about pay. This is strategic leadership, not analytical execution.
  • Executive access and board-level influence — You'll advise the C-suite and compensation committee directly. Your recommendations will shape workforce investment at the highest level.
  • The hardest problems in compensation — M&A integration, multi-geography architecture, executive compensation design, pay equity at scale. If you want the challenges that require the deepest expertise, they're here.

How to Apply

Submit your application through our careers page. Our process includes a conversation with the CHRO, a compensation strategy discussion, and an executive panel conversation. We move at a pace that respects your time — expect a decision within two weeks of your final interview.
