30-60-90 Day Onboarding: Junior HR Business Partner (IC2)

Overview

Level: IC2 — Junior HR Business Partner Focus: Building foundational HRBP capabilities, learning client groups, establishing reliable operational partnership Manager's Role: Structured mentor, active guide, regular feedback provider

IC2 HRBPs are developing professionals who need structured onboarding with clear expectations, frequent check-ins, and deliberate exposure to the full range of business partnership work. The goal is to build a strong operational foundation—reliable program execution, basic employee relations capability, and trust with frontline managers—while developing the judgment and business context that enable increasing independence over time.

Days 1-30: Learn

Theme: Build the Foundation

Primary Objectives: - Understand the organization's HR operating model, policies, and key programs - Learn the business context, goals, and team dynamics of assigned client groups - Build initial relationships with frontline managers and the senior HRBP partner - Develop working knowledge of HR systems, tools, and data sources

Week 1: Orientation & Context Setting

Day Focus Activities
1 Welcome HR team intros, org overview, role expectations, HRIS and tools access
2 HR Operations Walk through core HR processes—performance cycles, compensation planning, engagement surveys, case management
3 Client Groups Introduction to assigned business groups—org charts, leadership, business priorities, current people challenges
4 Employee Relations Overview of ER policies, case documentation standards, escalation protocols with senior HRBP
5 Stakeholder Intros Meet frontline managers in assigned groups, shadow senior HRBP in key meetings

Key Meetings to Schedule: - [ ] Manager 1:1 (weekly) - [ ] Senior HRBP partner (daily/weekly working sessions) - [ ] 1:1 with each HR team member (recruiting, comp, L&D, HR ops) - [ ] Frontline managers in assigned client groups - [ ] HR Centers of Excellence leads - [ ] Previous HRBP for assigned groups (if applicable)

Weeks 2-4: Immersion in Client Groups & HR Programs

Learning Goals: - [ ] Understand the business goals, operational rhythms, and key challenges of assigned client groups - [ ] Learn the end-to-end process for each major HR program (performance reviews, comp planning, engagement surveys) - [ ] Develop working proficiency in HRIS, case management tools, and reporting dashboards - [ ] Understand ER escalation protocols and documentation standards - [ ] Map key stakeholders and their expectations for HR support

Activities: - Shadow senior HRBP in manager coaching conversations and talent review meetings - Review recent ER cases in assigned groups to understand patterns and resolution approaches - Observe an engagement survey debrief or action-planning session - Complete any required compliance training and HR certification onboarding - Begin handling routine manager questions (policy clarifications, process guidance) with senior HRBP oversight

Milestones: - [ ] Can navigate HRIS and pull basic workforce reports for assigned groups - [ ] Familiar with active ER cases and ongoing people issues in assigned groups - [ ] Introduced to all frontline managers and established initial rapport - [ ] Understands the annual HR calendar and upcoming program milestones - [ ] Has observed at least two manager coaching or talent review sessions

30-Day Check-in

Manager Discussion Points: - How well do you understand your assigned client groups and their business context? - What have you learned about the HR programs and processes so far? - How is the working relationship with your senior HRBP partner developing? - What support or clarification do you need? - Initial feedback on contributions

Success Indicators: - [ ] Solid understanding of assigned client groups' structure and priorities - [ ] Working proficiency in HR systems and tools - [ ] Initial relationships built with frontline managers - [ ] Clear on ER escalation protocols and documentation standards - [ ] Comfortable asking questions and seeking guidance appropriately

Days 31-60: Contribute

Theme: Start Delivering

Primary Objectives: - Handle routine HR inquiries and manager questions independently - Support HR program execution with accurate, timely work - Begin managing simple employee relations cases with senior HRBP oversight - Build credibility with frontline managers through reliable follow-through

Weeks 5-6: Taking On Operational Ownership

Activities: - Own day-to-day HR inquiries from assigned client groups (policy questions, process guidance, basic data requests) - Prepare workforce data packages (headcount, attrition, hiring pipeline) for senior HRBP review - Support the current HR program cycle (performance review prep, compensation data collection, survey logistics) - Begin handling routine ER cases (interpersonal conflicts, policy questions, accommodation requests) with guidance - Draft manager communications and program rollout materials for assigned groups

Demonstrating Reliable Execution: - [ ] Manager questions answered accurately and promptly without requiring senior HRBP intervention on routine matters - [ ] Workforce data packages prepared with clean, accurate data and clear formatting - [ ] HR program tasks completed on time with minimal errors - [ ] ER case documentation meets quality standards with appropriate detail and objectivity - [ ] Communications to managers are professional, clear, and on-brand

Weeks 7-8: Building Independence & Judgment

Activities: - Take on more complex manager coaching conversations (onboarding guidance, development goal-setting, career discussions) - Collect and synthesize engagement data, exit interview themes, and pulse check insights for assigned groups - Coordinate with HR Centers of Excellence on program delivery and cross-functional questions - Identify process improvements for recurring HR operational tasks - Begin proactively flagging emerging people issues to senior HRBP

Growing Impact: - [ ] Frontline managers increasingly coming to you as first point of contact for routine people questions - [ ] Engagement and attrition data synthesized into actionable summaries that add value - [ ] Coaching managers on basic talent development topics with growing confidence - [ ] Identifying patterns in manager questions that suggest broader support needs - [ ] Working effectively with HR CoEs without requiring senior HRBP to broker connections

60-Day Check-in

Manager Discussion Points: - How confident are you handling routine manager inquiries independently? - What ER situations have you managed, and how did they go? - Where are you seeking guidance appropriately vs. where could you take more initiative? - How is your relationship with frontline managers developing? - Feedback on contributions and trajectory

Success Indicators: - [ ] Handling routine HR inquiries independently with accurate, timely responses - [ ] Supporting HR program execution reliably with minimal oversight - [ ] Managing simple ER cases with appropriate documentation and outcomes - [ ] Building trust with frontline managers through consistent follow-through - [ ] Contributing meaningful data and insights to senior HRBP's strategic work

Days 61-90: Own

Theme: Establish Yourself

Primary Objectives: - Operate as the reliable operational HRBP for assigned client groups - Manage routine ER cases independently with appropriate escalation of complex situations - Execute HR programs within assigned groups with high quality and minimal supervision - Demonstrate growing professional judgment in people-related situations

Weeks 9-10: Full Operational Ownership

Activities: - Own the full scope of operational HRBP work for assigned groups—inquiries, data, program execution, routine ER - Lead manager coaching conversations on standard topics (new hire onboarding, goal-setting, development planning) - Execute current HR program cycle deliverables (data preparation, communications, participation tracking) independently - Surface process improvement ideas and implement approved changes - Document and share best practices for recurring operational tasks

IC2 Expectations Met: - [ ] Frontline managers view you as a reliable first point of contact for day-to-day people questions - [ ] Workforce data gathered and organized accurately for senior HRBP review - [ ] Routine ER cases handled independently with proper documentation and timely resolution - [ ] HR programs deployed within assigned groups with high participation and clean data - [ ] Complex situations recognized and escalated with clear context and preliminary assessment

Weeks 11-12: Setting Long-Term Trajectory

Activities: - Build a development plan with your manager focused on strengthening consulting skills and business acumen - Identify areas where you want to deepen expertise (ER, talent management, organizational health) - Discuss IC3 path and the capabilities needed for independent business partnership - Solidify position and reputation as a dependable operational partner - Fully transition from "new hire" mindset

Looking Ahead: - [ ] Clear understanding of your operational scope and where it will grow - [ ] Development plan established with specific skill-building goals - [ ] Relationships with frontline managers solidified - [ ] Known as a reliable, responsive operational HRBP for assigned groups - [ ] Positioned for continued growth toward independent business partnership

90-Day Review

Manager Discussion Points: - Comprehensive onboarding assessment - Quality and reliability of operational HRBP work - ER judgment and escalation appropriateness - Business context understanding and growth trajectory - Recognition and development areas

Success Indicators: - [ ] Operating fully at IC2 level across assigned client groups - [ ] Operational ownership clearly established with minimal supervision needed - [ ] Professional judgment developing appropriately for ER and manager coaching - [ ] Strong working relationships with frontline managers and HR team - [ ] Clear on continued growth path toward IC3

Key Relationships to Build

Who Why When to Meet
Direct manager Alignment, feedback, growth Weekly
Senior HRBP partner Day-to-day guidance, escalation, mentorship Daily/weekly working sessions
Frontline managers in client groups Trust, operational partnership, issue surfacing Regular check-ins
HR Centers of Excellence (recruiting, comp, L&D) Program coordination, cross-functional questions First month, as needed
HR Operations/HRIS team Systems proficiency, data accuracy First month, as needed
Peer HRBPs Best practice sharing, support network Ongoing

Common IC2 Onboarding Challenges

Challenge Solution
Unclear on when to escalate vs. handle independently Establish clear escalation criteria with senior HRBP; review decisions weekly
Overwhelmed by volume of manager requests Prioritize with senior HRBP; build templates and playbooks for common questions
Struggling to build credibility with managers Focus on responsiveness and follow-through; small wins compound into trust
ER documentation not meeting standards Review exemplar cases; get feedback on early documentation before finalizing
Not understanding business context well enough Attend client group meetings; ask managers about their business priorities

Manager Checklist

Pre-Arrival

  • [ ] Assigned client groups identified
  • [ ] Senior HRBP partner confirmed and aligned on working model
  • [ ] HRIS and tools access provisioned
  • [ ] Initial manager meetings scheduled in client groups
  • [ ] First 30-day learning plan outlined

Week 1

  • [ ] HR team introductions complete
  • [ ] Client group orientation done
  • [ ] ER and escalation protocols reviewed
  • [ ] Tools and systems training initiated
  • [ ] Weekly cadence established with manager and senior HRBP

Ongoing

  • [ ] Operational ownership transferring progressively
  • [ ] ER case handling developing with appropriate oversight
  • [ ] Manager relationships forming in client groups
  • [ ] Professional judgment improving through coached experiences
  • [ ] Course corrections made as needed
