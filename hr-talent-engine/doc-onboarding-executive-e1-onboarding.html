30-60-90 Day Onboarding: Director of HR (E1)

Overview

Level: E1 — Director of HR Focus: Establishing domain ownership, building executive partnerships, developing senior HR leaders, connecting people strategy to business outcomes Manager's Role: Executive peer, providing strategic context and organizational access, partnering on the highest-stakes decisions

E1 Directors of HR are executive leaders who own a significant HR domain and operate at the intersection of people strategy and business outcomes. Onboarding focuses on understanding the domain's current state, assessing leadership bench strength, building executive-level partnerships, and developing a strategic vision that anticipates organizational needs 1-3 years ahead. The goal is to establish executive credibility quickly while taking full accountability for domain outcomes.

Days 1-30: Learn

Theme: Assess the Landscape

Primary Objectives: - Assess the domain's current state—program quality, team capabilities, compliance posture, and business impact - Build executive-level relationships with C-suite peers and cross-functional partners - Understand company strategy deeply enough to connect domain investments to business outcomes - Evaluate M3 leaders and the broader leadership bench within the domain

Week 1: Executive Immersion

Day Focus Activities
1 Welcome Executive team introductions, company strategy briefing, board context (if applicable)
2 Domain Review Assess domain programs, metrics, team structure, and current strategic priorities
3 Leadership Team Meet M2 and M3 leaders—evaluate capabilities, development needs, and strategic readiness
4 Business Context Meet business unit leaders and cross-functional executives—understand strategic needs and HR satisfaction
5 Strategic Alignment Deep-dive with CPO/CHRO on domain expectations, organizational priorities, and executive dynamics

Key Meetings to Schedule: - [ ] Manager 1:1 (weekly) - [ ] 1:1 with each M2 and M3 leader in the domain - [ ] Skip-level meetings with key M1 managers and senior HR professionals - [ ] C-suite executives (CEO, CFO, CTO, COO) - [ ] Finance, Legal, and Technology leadership - [ ] Board members or board committee chairs (if applicable and appropriate)

Weeks 2-4: Strategic Assessment

Learning Goals: - [ ] Comprehensive assessment of domain program quality, adoption, employee experience, and business impact - [ ] Clear view of M3 leadership capability and readiness for Director-level development - [ ] Understanding of how the domain connects to and depends on other HR domains - [ ] Assessment of where domain investments create competitive advantage vs. merely operational compliance - [ ] Map of executive relationships, organizational dynamics, and decision-making patterns at the leadership level

Activities: - Review domain metrics, program effectiveness data, and business impact measures - Assess the leadership bench—M3s ready for growth, M2s with Director potential, succession gaps - Evaluate domain technology, analytics capabilities, and infrastructure investments - Understand the regulatory and compliance landscape relevant to the domain - Develop initial strategic assessment connecting domain capabilities to business needs

Milestones: - [ ] Leadership cadence established with all direct reports - [ ] Can articulate domain strengths, gaps, and strategic opportunities with executive fluency - [ ] Executive relationships initiated—viewed as a credible leadership addition - [ ] Understanding of company financial dynamics, competitive positioning, and strategic priorities - [ ] Initial strategic assessment developed (for manager discussion and executive alignment)

30-Day Check-in

Manager Discussion Points: - What is your assessment of the domain's strategic health and business impact? - Where does the domain create competitive advantage, and where does it merely maintain compliance? - How do you evaluate the leadership bench—M3s, M2s, and succession strength? - What executive partnerships need investment? - Initial feedback on executive presence and contributions

Success Indicators: - [ ] Deep understanding of domain across programs, people, and business impact - [ ] Domain leadership team trusts and is energized by the new executive - [ ] Executive relationships established with credibility and mutual respect - [ ] Identified highest-priority strategic and organizational investments - [ ] Operating with executive judgment and strategic perspective

Days 31-60: Contribute

Theme: Shape Direction

Primary Objectives: - Develop M3 leaders toward Director-level capability with deliberate coaching - Begin shaping domain strategy that connects people investments to business outcomes - Make strategic trade-offs with company-level perspective - Build executive partnerships that position the domain as a strategic asset

Weeks 5-6: Establishing Executive Ownership

Activities: - Set strategic direction for the domain with clear connection to business objectives - Initiate development plans for M3 leaders that build strategic capability, not just operational excellence - Make consequential decisions about domain priorities, investments, and resource allocation - Contribute to company strategy discussions from a people-informed perspective - Address the highest-priority program quality or coordination gap in the domain

Demonstrating Executive Leadership: - [ ] Domain strategy articulated with clarity and business connection—not just HR language - [ ] M3 leaders receiving coaching that develops strategic thinking and executive readiness - [ ] Strategic trade-offs made with company-level perspective, not just domain optimization - [ ] Cross-functional executive partnerships producing tangible collaboration - [ ] Domain team feeling increased clarity of purpose and strategic direction

Weeks 7-8: Driving Strategic Impact

Activities: - Lead a strategic initiative that demonstrates domain impact on business outcomes - Build executive alignment on people investments within the domain - Strengthen Finance, Legal, and Technology partnerships on shared strategic priorities - Develop the leadership bench—identify succession candidates and create development opportunities - Represent the domain in executive forums and company strategic discussions

Growing Impact: - [ ] Domain strategy gaining executive support and organizational momentum - [ ] M3 leaders growing toward greater strategic capability and executive readiness - [ ] Cross-functional executive partnerships deepening beyond coordination to strategic alignment - [ ] People investments in the domain demonstrating connection to business outcomes - [ ] Domain positioned as a source of competitive advantage, not just operational necessity

60-Day Check-in

Manager Discussion Points: - How is domain strategy taking shape, and how does it connect to company objectives? - What progress are M3 leaders making in their development? - How are executive partnerships evolving? - Where do you need more authority, resources, or organizational support? - Feedback on executive leadership and strategic impact

Success Indicators: - [ ] Domain strategy articulated and gaining organizational alignment - [ ] M3 leader development underway with visible trajectory - [ ] Executive partnerships producing strategic value beyond coordination - [ ] At least one initiative demonstrating domain impact on business outcomes - [ ] Operating with full executive accountability for domain performance

Days 61-90: Own

Theme: Drive Strategic Impact

Primary Objectives: - Operate fully at E1 level—full accountability for domain outcomes and strategic direction - Shape company strategy through active executive contribution - Demonstrate sustained strategic impact through the leadership team - Establish long-term trajectory for the domain and senior leadership development

Weeks 9-10: Full E1 Operation

Activities: - Own domain outcomes completely—program effectiveness, business impact, and team performance - Drive strategic planning for the domain with 1-3 year horizons - Manage the leadership team—develop M3s, assess M2s, build succession depth - Lead cross-functional executive initiatives that improve company-wide outcomes - Contribute to company strategy as an executive participant with people-informed perspective

E1 Expectations Met: - [ ] Domain delivering measurable business value with clear connection between people investments and company outcomes - [ ] Strategic vision for the domain anticipating organizational needs 1-3 years ahead - [ ] M3 leaders growing toward Director-level capability through deliberate development - [ ] Executive partnerships with Finance, Technology, and Operations driving company-wide outcomes - [ ] Full accountability owned—program effectiveness, adoption, and business impact without requiring escalation

Weeks 11-12: Setting Long-Term Trajectory

Activities: - Develop domain strategic roadmap for the next 1-3 years - Establish succession plans for leadership roles within the domain - Discuss E2 development path with your manager - Solidify position and reputation as an executive leader - Fully transition from "new hire" mindset

Looking Ahead: - [ ] Clear strategic vision for the domain with executive and organizational alignment - [ ] Development plan established for your own executive growth - [ ] Relationships solidified across the executive team and key stakeholders - [ ] Known as a strategic executive who drives business outcomes through people - [ ] Positioned for continued impact at the senior executive level

90-Day Review

Manager Discussion Points: - Comprehensive onboarding assessment - Domain ownership and strategic direction evaluation - Senior leader development and bench strength assessment - Executive influence and cross-functional impact observed - Path forward (deepening E1 domain impact vs. broadening toward E2) - Recognition and growth areas

Success Indicators: - [ ] Operating fully at E1 level with strategic authority and executive credibility - [ ] Domain ownership clearly established with measurable business impact - [ ] Senior leader development demonstrating executive multiplier effect - [ ] Strong executive partnerships and domain positioned as strategic asset - [ ] Clear on continued growth path

Key Relationships to Build

Who Why When to Meet
CPO/CHRO Strategic alignment, executive counsel, domain authority Weekly
M2 and M3 leaders Development, delegation, domain health Weekly
CEO Company strategy, organizational priorities, executive credibility First month, monthly
CFO Budget, people investments, shared analytics and business cases First month, biweekly or monthly
CTO/COO Cross-functional strategy, organizational alignment First month, monthly
Business unit leaders Workforce needs, domain impact, strategic partnership First month, ongoing

Common E1 Onboarding Challenges

Challenge Solution
Transitioning from senior management to executive leadership mindset Focus on strategy and influence, not operational management; resist the pull to go deep on execution
Inheriting M3 leaders with uneven strategic readiness Assess honestly; invest in development for high-potential leaders; address gaps directly
Executive team unsure of domain's strategic value Connect every conversation to business outcomes; demonstrate strategic thinking; deliver early wins with business impact
Balancing domain depth with company-wide perspective Let M3 leaders own domain depth; focus your energy on strategic connections and executive influence
Building executive partnerships from a new position Lead with listening and strategic value; demonstrate sound judgment before pushing bold initiatives
Board-level exposure when not yet established Prepare thoroughly; focus on business impact language; build confidence through smaller executive forums first
