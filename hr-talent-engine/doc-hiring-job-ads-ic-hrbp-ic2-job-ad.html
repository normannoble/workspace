Junior HR Business Partner — Human Resources

The Opportunity

You'll step into the world of strategic HR by partnering directly with frontline managers to solve real people challenges — handling employee relations cases, supporting talent reviews, deploying HR programs, and building the credibility that turns an HR professional into a trusted business advisor. Working alongside a senior HRBP, you'll develop the pattern recognition, professional judgment, and business acumen that are the foundation of a strategic HR career. This isn't back-office HR; you're embedded in the business, learning how people strategies connect to operational outcomes. If you're ready to move from HR generalist work into genuine business partnership — with mentorship and room to grow — this is where it starts.

What You'll Do

  • Become the go-to person for frontline managers — You'll build trust with managers in your assigned business groups by being responsive, following through, and providing practical guidance on day-to-day people questions. This is the foundation for deeper strategic partnership as your expertise grows.
  • Handle employee relations with growing judgment — You'll independently manage routine ER cases — interpersonal conflicts, policy questions, accommodation requests, and low-complexity performance situations — with appropriate outcomes, proper documentation, and timely resolution. Complex cases go to your senior partner, but you'll learn from every one.
  • Make talent processes actually work — You'll prepare talent review data, support calibration sessions, coach managers on development conversations, and flag performance concerns early — ensuring the talent management rhythm in your client groups is more than a compliance exercise.
  • Deploy HR programs with high adoption — You'll drive execution of performance review cycles, compensation planning, engagement surveys, and benefits enrollment within your assigned groups — answering questions, collecting feedback, and ensuring clean data and high participation.

What You Bring

  • 2-5 years of progressive HR experience, including some exposure to generalist or HRBP work (not purely specialist or administrative)
  • Experience handling routine employee relations cases with appropriate documentation and outcomes
  • Foundational knowledge across core HR disciplines — employee relations, talent management, compensation basics, and employment law fundamentals
  • Relationship-building skills — you establish credibility with managers through responsiveness, follow-through, and practical guidance
  • Organizational skills to manage multiple concurrent HR processes across assigned groups without dropping threads
  • Written and verbal communication appropriate for HR contexts — you document ER cases clearly and draft professional manager communications
  • Discretion and integrity in handling confidential information

Nice to Have

  • Experience supporting talent review or calibration processes, even in a data-preparation capacity
  • Hands-on experience with HRIS platforms and HR reporting tools
  • HR certification (SHRM-CP, PHR) or related credentials

Why [Company]

  • Real business partnership, not back-office HR — You'll be embedded with business teams, solving real people problems and seeing how your work connects to operational outcomes. This is applied HR.
  • Mentorship that builds strategic judgment — You'll work alongside a senior HRBP who will involve you in complex situations, explain the reasoning behind decisions, and deliberately develop your pattern recognition for the ambiguous calls that define great HR.
  • A clear path from junior to strategic partner — Every ER case, talent review, and manager coaching conversation builds the foundation for the strategic HRBP role you're growing into. Your development is planned, not left to chance.

How to Apply

Submit your application through our careers page. Our process includes a conversation with the senior HRBP you'd partner with, a practical scenario exercise focused on employee relations judgment and stakeholder communication, and a team fit conversation. We move quickly and keep you informed — expect a decision within two weeks of your final interview.
